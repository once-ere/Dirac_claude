\section{The exact block reduction}

\textbf{Ansatz.} $\Psi = e^{ik\cdot x}e^{-(3/2)Hy}e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}\chi(y,x_4)$ gives EXACTLY, for a time-dependent $a_4$ and $b_4$,

\[
\tfrac1f\gamma^0\partial_y\chi + \gamma^4\partial_{x_4}\chi + i\kappa\,k\cdot\gamma\,\chi = (M_{\mathrm{eff}} - iv_v\gamma^4)\chi ,\qquad \kappa(x_4) = e^{-a_4(x_4)},\qquad f(x_4) = e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2} ,
\]

i.e. $i\partial_{x_4}\chi = h(x_4)\chi$ with $h$ Hermitian in $\int dy\,\chi^\dagger\chi$. The factor $e^{-(3/2)Hy}$ removes the term $\tfrac32(H/f)\gamma^0$ and the factor $e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}$ the term $(b_4'/b_4 - \tfrac12a_4')\gamma^4$; without either the term survives. At a fixed instant the instantaneous (adiabatic) problem is $h(a_{4,0}, f_0)\chi = \varepsilon\chi$ with $a_{4,0} = a_4(x_4)$ and $f_0 = f(x_4)$.

\textbf{Blocks.} $J = \gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^4$ ($J^2 = 1$), $K_1 = \gamma^2\gamma^3$ and $K_2 = \gamma^5\gamma^6$ ($K^2 = -1$) commute with each other and with $\gamma^0$, $\gamma^0\gamma^1$, $\gamma^0\gamma^4$, $B$ and $C$; their joint eigenspaces, labelled $(j, s_2, s_3)$ with $j, s_2, s_3 = \pm1$, are eight rank-2 blocks with an explicit unitary basis $V$ (entries of $2\sqrt2\,V$ in $\{0, \pm1, \pm i\}$). For $k$ along $x_1$ (the spinor rotation commutes with $\gamma^0$, $\gamma^4$, $B$, $C$, so the spectra depend on $|k|$ only), every block of type $j$ carries

\[
h_j = j\big[-\tfrac{i}{f}\sigma_1\,\tfrac{d}{dy} + M_{\mathrm{eff}}(y)\sigma_2 + \kappa k\sigma_3\big] + v_v(y) ,
\]

equivalently $\chi' = fN\chi$ with $N = M_{\mathrm{eff}}\sigma_3 - \kappa k\sigma_2 + ij(\varepsilon - v_v)\sigma_1$; in the real form $\chi = (a, ib)$, $a' = f[M_{\mathrm{eff}}a - (\kappa k + j(\varepsilon - v_v))b]$ and $b' = f[(j(\varepsilon - v_v) - \kappa k)a - M_{\mathrm{eff}}b]$. In the block basis $\gamma^0 = \sigma_3$, $B = js_2$ and $C = s_2\sigma_2$.

\textbf{Symmetries and degeneracies.} $h_{-1} - v = -(h_{+1} - v)$ and $\sigma_3h_j(k)\sigma_3 = h_{-j}(-k)$: the $(j,k)$ and $(-j,-k)$ orbitals are degenerate. On a lattice shell $|k|^2 = \Delta k^2n_2$ with $r_3(n_2)$ vectors every level of $h_j(|k|)$ is $4r_3(n_2)$-fold for each $j$; at $k = 0$ every level is 4-fold per $j$. The chirality $\Gamma$ maps block $(j, s_2, s_3)$ onto $(-j, s_2, s_3)$ with the $2 \times 2$ entry $s_2\sigma_2$, and $\sigma_2h_j(M,k)\sigma_2 = h_{-j}(-M,k)$ with the two fold conditions exchanged (the ingredient of T3, section 12).

\textbf{Where the deflation enters (exact).} $a_{4,0}$ and $b_{4,0}$ enter $h_j$ through $\kappa k = ke^{-a_{4,0}}$ (the 3-momenta redshift as $ke^{-a_4}$) and through the hidden scale $f_0 = e^{-a_{4,0}}b_{4,0}^{1/2}$, which multiplies the hidden derivative by $1/f_0$ (the proper hidden length of the cut-off cell is $f_0L$); because the good sector has no extra-time momentum, the extra times themselves enter only through the proper cell volume, that is through the densities and the mean field (sections 2.1 and 10).

\section{The functional: Hartree, exact uniform-gas exchange, Mermin}

\textbf{Exchange (exact for the uniform gas).} The interaction is $U = \frac{\lambda}{2}S^2$, $S = \bar\Psi\Psi$, normal ordered ($\lambda > 0$ repulsive). For a quasi-free state, Wick's theorem gives $\langle :S^2: \rangle = (\mathrm{Tr}\,C\rho)^2 - \mathrm{Tr}(C\rho C\rho)$. For every $p \to -p$ symmetric occupation of the 8-fold good-sector gas, at every temperature, $\rho = (nB + SC)/16$ and

\[
e_H = \tfrac{\lambda}{2}S^2 ,\qquad e_x = -\tfrac{\lambda}{32}\big(n^2 + S^2\big) ,\qquad
e_{\mathrm{int}} = \tfrac{15}{32}\lambda S^2 - \tfrac{1}{32}\lambda n^2 ,
\]

\[
M_{\mathrm{eff}}(y) = m + \tfrac{15}{16}\lambda S(y) ,\qquad v_v(y) = -\tfrac{\lambda}{16}n(y) ,
\]

with proper densities; for one filled level at rest $E_x/E_H = -1/8$, and on shell $\langle\mathcal{L}\rangle/\sqrt{|g|} = e_{\mathrm{int}}$. In the solvers' units (section 2.1) the same formulas hold with $\lambda \to \hat\lambda$ and every density per $\mathrm{Vol}_7(a_{4,0})$. No correlation is included (Hartree plus exchange only). The Rust solver reads the coefficients from \texttt{ks-theor\allowbreak{}y.json} and checks them (\texttt{theory\_i\allowbreak{}nput\_coe\allowbreak{}fficient\allowbreak{}s}: $M_{\mathrm{eff}} = m + 0.9375\lambda S$, $v_v = -0.0625\lambda n$).

\textbf{Exact Fock exchange of the slab (diagnostic and variant).} For closed shells the exact Fock exchange of the Kohn-Sham determinant is also local, $e_x^{\mathrm{exact}} = -\frac{\lambda}{32}(n^2 + S^2 - Q^2 - Y^2)$ with $Q = \langle\Psi^\dagger B\gamma^0\Psi\rangle$ and $Y = 0$; the canonical functional is the uniform-gas one, the difference $\Delta E_x = \frac{\lambda}{32}\,2\mathrm{Vol}_7\int e^{3Hy}Q^2\,dy$ is reported for every state, and the exact-Fock functional is solved as a variant (60 states, \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/kohn\_sh\allowbreak{}am/resul\allowbreak{}ts/exx/e\allowbreak{}xact-foc\allowbreak{}k-varian\allowbreak{}t.csv}).

\textbf{Densities, energy, Mermin.} Per orbital, with $P(y) = e^{-3Hy}/\mathrm{Vol}_7(a_{4,0})$: $n_o = P\chi^\dagger\chi$, $s_o = P\chi^\dagger j\sigma_2\chi$, $t_o = P\chi^\dagger j\sigma_3\chi$, $q_o = P\chi^\dagger\sigma_3\chi$. Totals are $\sum w\,g\,f$ times these, $g = 4r_3(n_2)$, $w = 1/2$ (section 5). The Kohn-Sham energy of the doubled system is $E_{KS} = \sum g f\varepsilon - 2\mathrm{Vol}_7\int_{-L}^{0}e^{3Hy}e_{\mathrm{int}}\,dy$; Mermin's occupations are $f = 1/(1 + e^{(\varepsilon-\mu)/T})$ with $\mu$ fixed by $\sum g f = N$, the entropy is $S_{\mathrm{ent}} = -\sum g[f\ln f + (1-f)\ln(1-f)]$, the grand potential (the thermodynamic effective potential) is $\Omega = -T\sum g\ln(1 + e^{-(\varepsilon-\mu)/T}) - 2\mathrm{Vol}_7\int_{-L}^{0}e^{3Hy}e_{\mathrm{int}}\,dy$, and $F = \Omega + \mu N$.

\section{Boundary conditions, filling convention, numerics and parameters}

\textbf{Fold (both fold conditions filled, ASSUMED).} The fold $\cos z = 0$ at $y = 0$ joins the two halves of a cell; the reduced equation is the same on both halves ($dy = \cot z\,dx_0$, $e^{x_0}dx_0 = f\,dy$, $\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu$ free of $x_0$), so a spinor continuous across the fold has the same $\chi(y)$ on both halves; the boundary form of the doubled cell at the fold is $2(-ij/f)\phi^\dagger\sigma_1\chi(0)$, and self-adjointness needs $(1 - Q(\theta))\chi(0) = 0$. The record fills both fold conditions, the even $\theta = 0$ ($\chi_2(0) = 0$, $b(0) = 0$) and the odd $\theta = \pi$ ($\chi_1(0) = 0$, $a(0) = 0$), each normalised on one half; $N$ is the particle number of the whole cell, each half holds $N/2$, and $w = 1/2$ per half. That both are filled is ASSUMED (the record's convention); there is no mirror copy.

\textbf{Tip (chosen).} At $y = -L$: $(1 - Q(\theta))\chi(-L) = 0$, $Q(\theta) = \cos\theta\,\sigma_3 + \sin\theta\,\sigma_2$, canonical $\theta = 0$, cutoff $L = 3$. With $\kappa$ independent of $y$ nothing suppresses an orbital toward the tip: for constant $M$ and $v$ the branches are $e^{\pm f\lambda y}$, $\lambda = \sqrt{M^2 + \kappa^2k^2 - (\varepsilon - v)^2}$. The dependence on $L$ is measured in \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/kohn\_sh\allowbreak{}am/tip\_c\allowbreak{}onvergen\allowbreak{}ce/} ($L = 3$ to $6$ in steps of $0.5$ with the record's step $h = 1/300$, every run repeated at $h/2$; a copy of the solver that reads $L$ from the environment reproduces the committed quick matrix byte for byte at $L = 3$; the reference solver agrees at $L = 3$ and $4$; report \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/kohn\_sh\allowbreak{}am/tip\_c\allowbreak{}onvergen\allowbreak{}ce/tip-c\allowbreak{}onvergen\allowbreak{}ce.json}, 7 of 7 checks PASS; rerun for the corrected metric on 2026-10-17). The free states are exact fold-band gases and do not depend on $L$: $E_{KS}$, HOMO, LUMO and the gap of every free state with $N = 8$ and $592$ and of $N = 2352$ at $a_{4,0} = 1, 2$ are the same at every $L$; only the values at the fold change, through the normalisation of the fold-band orbital, $\rho(0; L) = \rho(0; \infty)/(1 - e^{-2fmL})$ (the recorded $L = 3$ fold values are high by 0.25\%, 1.38\% and 4.81\% at $a_{4,0} = 0, 1, 2$). The $k = 0$ bulk levels approach $m$ only algebraically, like the levels of a box of proper length $fL$: the bulk edge 1.2923 that defines $N = 2352$ is an $L = 3$ value (1.0977 at $L = 6$), and the free $N = 2352$, $a_{4,0} = 0$ state changes its occupied set from $L = 3.5$ on (its gap 0.01754 closes; $E_{KS} = 2288.388$ at $L = 3$ and $2245.486$ at $L = 6$, not converged). The interacting zero modes depend on $L$, the more the later the slice: their proper density grows toward the tip like $e^{(3H - 2fm)|y|}$, and for $N = 8$ the recorded $E_{KS}$ is $\mp 3.7787\times10^{-2}$ against $\mp 3.8688\times10^{-2}$ at large $L$ at $a_{4,0} = 0$, $\mp 1.4571\times10^{-2}$ against $\mp 2.1622\times10^{-2}$ at $a_{4,0} = 1$ and $\mp 6.980\times10^{-3}$ against $\mp 3.239\times10^{-2}$ at $a_{4,0} = 2$ ($\hat\lambda = \pm\lambda_1$; 0.977, 0.674 and 0.215 of the large-$L$ values); the interaction shifts $E_{KS}(\pm\lambda_1) - E_{KS}(0)$ of $N = 592$ and $2352$ at $a_{4,0} = 2$ are 4.6 to 4.7 times larger at large $L$ than at $L = 3$. 17 of the 19 interacting states studied converge (geometric tails); the self-consistent iteration fails only for $N = 2352$ at $a_{4,0} = 0$, from $L = 3.5$ for $+\lambda_1$ and from $L = 4.5$ for $-\lambda_1$ (a failure of the iteration, not a proof that no self-consistent state exists). The record's calibration rule applied at each $L$ drives $\lambda_1$ to zero: $157.7$, $2.131$ and $0.5363$ at $L = 3$, $1.249$, $0.01687$ and $1.03\times10^{-5}$ at $L = 6$ ($N = 8, 592, 2352$).

\textbf{Filling (CONVENTION, justification OPEN).} Particles occupy the positive branch (the levels with $\varepsilon > 0$ of the $\lambda = 0$ problem) and the $k = 0$ fold zero modes, each followed continuously in $\lambda$; the negative branch is the normal-ordered sea and contributes nothing to $N$, $n$, $S$ or the energy-momentum tensor. \section{Adiabaticity and Fermi-level crossings}

Exactly (mean field), $i\partial_{x_4}\chi = h(a_4(x_4), f(x_4))\chi$ conserves $k$, the block and the fold condition; transitions occur only between levels of the same $(k, j, \text{block}, \text{fold condition})$. The measure along $a_4 = AHx_4$ is

\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_{nm} &= AH\,\frac{\lvert\langle n\rvert\partial_ah\lvert m\rangle\rvert}{(\varepsilon_n - \varepsilon_m)^2} = AH\,\frac{\lvert\langle n\vert\partial_am\rangle\rvert}{\lvert\varepsilon_n - \varepsilon_m\rvert},\\
\partial_ah_j &= -j\kappa k\sigma_3 + \big(1 - \tfrac{\beta}{2}\big)\,j\big(-\tfrac{i}{f}\sigma_1\partial_y\big) + j(\partial_aM_{\mathrm{eff}})\sigma_2 + \partial_av_v ,
\end{aligned}
\]

for $n$ occupied and $m$ empty in the same sector (the second term, one third of the kinetic term, comes from the shrinking hidden direction); the state is adiabatic if $\max Q_{nm} \ll 1$, and $Q_{nm}^2$ is the leading-order transition probability. \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/SPEC.md} section 7 names the Kohn-Sham energy-momentum tensor as the source of the field equations for $a_4$ and $b_4$. The Einstein-Lovelock equations of the corrected metric (\texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/field\_e\allowbreak{}quations\allowbreak{}\_a4}, key generalSource of \texttt{a4-equat\allowbreak{}ions.jso\allowbreak{}n}) require of any source: (C1) every component independent of $x_0$ (the left-hand sides contain no $x_0$ for every $a_4$, $b_4$); (C2) $p_0 = p_t$ (the hidden and the extra-time components of every left-hand side are equal); (C3) no off-diagonal component, $T^{x_4}{}_{x_0} = 0$; (C4) on the history $a_4 = Hx_4$, $b_4 = e^{(4/3)Hx_4}$ with the author's weights and $\Lambda = -10$, the source $\kappa T - \Lambda$ that \texttt{a4-equat\allowbreak{}ions.jso\allowbreak{}n} records for this history (key exponentialHistory, author's weights), a function of $k = e^{(2/3)Hx_4}$ with $p_0 = p_t$. \begin{enumerate}

\section{What is proved, computed, assumed and not established}

\subsection{Proved (exact, two independent verifiers)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The block reduction (section 3): for every history $a_4(x_4)$, $b_4(x_4)$ the good-sector equation reduces exactly to eight $2 \times 2$ blocks $h_j$ in the hidden coordinate; the spin-connection terms $\tfrac32(H/f)\gamma^0$ and $(b_4'/b_4 - \tfrac12a_4')\gamma^4$ are removed by the half-density factor $e^{-(3/2)Hy}e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}$; the hidden direction enters through $1/f$ and the 3-momenta through the constant $\kappa = e^{-a_4}$.
\item The exchange functional (section 4): the exact local exchange of the uniform 8-fold good-sector gas at every temperature, $M_{\mathrm{eff}} = m + \frac{15}{16}\lambda S$, $v_v = -\lambda n/16$; the exact Fock exchange of the closed-shell slab differs by $+\lambda Q^2/32$; a state depends on the coupling and the cell volume only through their ratio.
\item The boundary-value structure (section 5): self-adjointness with current-killing ends, the fold conditions, the exact $k = 0$ spectra, the fold band $\varepsilon = \kappa k$ and its slope.
\item The rescaling identity (section 10), the energy-momentum tensor of the Kohn-Sham states with its trace and conservation identities and the energy change along the history (section 9), and the adiabaticity identity of the measure (section 7).
\item T3 (section 12; proved in \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/docs/PA\allowbreak{}IR\_CREAT\allowbreak{}ION\_PROO\allowbreak{}FS.md}), under its stated hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Computed (numerical, two independent solvers, cross-checked)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The instantaneous ground states, first excited states (gap, $\Delta$-SCF, particle-hole lists), Mermin states, energy-momentum profiles, rescaling partners and exact-Fock variants of the canonical matrix (sections 6 to 10), with the Rust solver and the reference agreeing on the full matrix within the stated uncertainty (31 of 31 checks, 217571 comparisons).
\item The adiabaticity measure ($Q_{\max} \leq 0.1982205$) and the absence of Fermi-level crossings in the canonical matrix; the crossing demonstration at $N = 2360$ (section 7).
\item That the recorded states are not admissible sources of the $a_4$ and $b_4$ equations (section 11).
\item The dependence on the tip cutoff $L$ from 3 to 6 of 29 recorded states, the 27 ground states with $N \in \{8, 592, 2352\}$, $\lambda \in \{0, \pm\lambda_1\}$, $a_{4,0} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ and two Mermin states of $N = 592$ ($\lambda = 0$, $a_{4,0} = 2$, $T = 0.05$; $\lambda = \lambda_1$, $a_{4,0} = 1$, $T = 0.02$) (section 5; \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/kohn\_sh\allowbreak{}am/tip\_c\allowbreak{}onvergen\allowbreak{}ce/}, 7 of 7 checks PASS; the Rust solver at every $L$, the reference at $L = 3$ and $4$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Assumed or chosen}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Both fold conditions filled at the fold $z = \pi/2$, where the two halves of a cell meet (ASSUMED); the metric is degenerate there and no junction condition is derived.
\item The regular tip at the cutoff $L = 3$ with $\theta = 0$ (chosen). Its effect is measured (section 5): the free fold-band gases do not depend on $L$, except their values at the fold (the normalisation of the fold-band orbital) and except $N = 2352$ at $a_{4,0} = 0$; the interaction energies do: the recorded $N = 8$ interaction energy is 0.977, 0.674 and 0.215 of its large-$L$ value at $a_{4,0} = 0, 1, 2$. The couplings (calibrated at $L = 3$) and $N = 2352$ (bulk edge at $L = 3$) are themselves $L = 3$ choices.
\item The filling convention (particles on the positive branch and on the $k = 0$ zero modes; the sea not populated thermally), whose justification is OPEN; the sea-hole diagnostic shows where it fails (27 of 135 thermal states).
\item The history $a_4 = AHx_4$, $b_4 = b_4(0)e^{(4/3)AHx_4}$, $A = 1$, PRESCRIBED, not solved for.
\item The parameters of section 5 ($N$, $\hat\lambda$, $L$, $\Delta k$, $v_t$, $T$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Not established}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The time-dependent (non-adiabatic) Kohn-Sham problem (TDDFT) is OPEN: only instantaneous states are computed; the measure $Q_{\max}$ is a consistency indicator, not a solution of the evolution.
\item Correlation: the functional is Hartree plus exchange only; no correlation functional is derived or used.
\item Back-reaction: the Kohn-Sham states are a test field on a prescribed background; no recorded state is an admissible source of the field equations for $a_4$ and $b_4$, and no history $a_4(x_4)$, $b_4(x_4)$ is derived from the Kohn-Sham source.
\item Equations of state and the dark-sector hypothesis are not part of this document (\texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/dark\_se\allowbreak{}ctor/dir\allowbreak{}ac16comp\allowbreak{}lex}).
\item No creation process, rate or amplitude for universes follows from the Kohn-Sham model or from T3.
\item A thermal treatment of the Dirac sea (pairs) where the particle-only ensemble fails; states beyond the computed range $a_{4,0} \in [0, 2]$; the continuum limit $\Delta k \to 0$; Kohn-Sham states with extra-time momenta. The limit $L \to \infty$ is established (numerically, with uncertainties) only for the states and quantities marked converged in \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/kohn\_sh\allowbreak{}am/tip\_c\allowbreak{}onvergen\allowbreak{}ce/READM\allowbreak{}E.md}; it is NOT established for $N = 2352$ at $a_{4,0} = 0$ (the $k = 0$ bulk levels approach $m$ algebraically and the occupied set changes; the iteration of its interacting partners fails), nor for the Mermin state N592\_lam0\_a20\_T50 (successive changes of about $10^{-8}$ relative that do not meet the geometric-tail rule). It is not computed at $L \neq 3$ for $\lambda_2$, the slices 0.5 and 1.5, $T = 0.01$ and the other thermal states, the excited states and the adiabaticity measure, nor at $L > 6$. The record's calibration rule drives $\lambda_1$ to zero as $L$ grows.
\end{enumerate}
