\begin{abstract}
This document records, for the primordial gravitational field of \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/SPEC.md} section 1, the field theory of dirac16complex00: a field $\Phi$ with 16 complex commuting components that transform as a spinor of Pin(4,4) (a set of 16 scalar fields that transform as a Pin(4,4) spinor), treated as a classical ("semi-classical") field. The metric is the corrected metric of 2026-10-16 (version 2): $g = \mathrm{diag}\big(e^{-2a_4}b_4\cot^2 z,\ e^{2a_4}\,(\times3),\ -1,\ -e^{-2a_4}b_4(\sin^2 z)^{1/6}\,(\times3)\big)$ with $z = 6Hx_0$ real, two functions $a_4(x_4)$ and $b_4(x_4) > 0$ of the time, the hidden coordinate $x_0$ space-like and $g_{44} = -1$; every statement is proved for general $a_4$ and $b_4$ and for every real $z$ (the metric of 2026-10-14 and its version 1 of 2026-10-16 are withdrawn). The document gives the Lagrangian and its coupling to gravity through the vielbein and the canonical spin connection, the exact Euler-Lagrange equations (covariant, explicit in the metric, and as 16 component equations), the non-triviality theorem [2] with its proof and its exact scope (in the diagonal vielbein the gravitational term of the field equation is $\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu = \tfrac32He^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}\gamma^{(x_0)} + (b_4'/b_4 - \tfrac12a_4')\gamma^{(x_4)}$, never zero for $H > 0$; this value depends on the frame and on the field variables, while $\Omega_\mu$ itself vanishes in no frame wherever the metric is curved, which it is on the background history), the self-consistency of the equations and its limits (no well-posed Cauchy problem for data that depend on the extra times; boundary terms at the degenerate points $z = \pi/2 + n\pi$), the energy-momentum tensor as a classical bilinear, its kinetic and potential parts, the energy density, the pressures and the equations of state, and the Einstein-Lovelock field equations for $a_4(x_4)$ and $b_4(x_4)$ with dirac16complex00 as the source. Every formula is an output of Revision code (Wolfram and an independent sympy checker for each part); each section ends with the names of the checks that verify it, and the check index collects them. Nothing is taken from the earlier stages of the repository. The classical energy of dirac16complex00 is unbounded below already for $U = 0$ (positive-frequency modes of negative energy) and its charge is indefinite and only locally conserved. The field equations admit no off-diagonal source and require equal hidden and extra-time pressures; a homogeneous condensate has equal pressures in all directions, and the background history of SPEC section 1 requires an anisotropic source that grows with $x_4$, so no condensate is its source. What the record does not establish is stated in the section "What is not claimed". The verdict of the dark-sector investigation of Hypothesis00 is summarised in section 10, and section 1 points to the other Revision documents.

\section{The field dirac16complex00 and its symmetry group}

dirac16complex00 is a field $\Phi$ with 16 complex commuting components (16 scalar fields) that transform as a Pin(4,4) spinor. It is a classical field; it is not quantised (SPEC section 6: only dirac16complex, the anticommuting field, is quantised canonically). SPEC section 3 calls it the analogue of Dirac's 1928 wave function and the author calls it semi-classical; both are interpretations, and the record shows two exact differences from Dirac's wave function: its charge $Q = \int\sqrt{|g|}\,\Phi^\dagger B\Phi\,d^7x$ (locally conserved, section 7) is an indefinite form, and its classical energy is unbounded below already for $U = 0$ in the good sector (section 9).

\textbf{The gammas.} The real $16\times16$ matrices are the author's T16, rebuilt in Revision code from the author's formulas (the tau matrices and their blocks), with the frame map $\gamma^{(x_k)} = T16[k]$ for $k = 0, \dots, 7$ ($\gamma^{(x_0)}$ of the hidden direction, $\gamma^{(x_4)}$ of the time). Verified exactly:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\{\gamma^{(a)}, \gamma^{(b)}\} = 2\eta^{ab}$ with $\eta = \mathrm{diag}(+1,+1,+1,+1,-1,-1,-1,-1)$ in the order $x_0 \ldots x_7$; every $\gamma^{(a)}$ is a real signed permutation matrix; the space-like ones are symmetric, the time-like ones antisymmetric.
\item $C = \gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_1)}\gamma^{(x_2)}\gamma^{(x_3)}$ is real symmetric, $C^2 = 1$, and $C\gamma^{(a)}$ is real antisymmetric for every $a$; $C = \mathrm{diag}(-\sigma, \sigma)$ with the notebook's $\sigma$.
\item $\Gamma = \gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_1)}\cdots\gamma^{(x_7)} = \mathrm{diag}(-I_8, I_8)$, $\Gamma^2 = 1$, and $\Gamma$ anticommutes with every $\gamma^{(a)}$.
\item $B = -iC\gamma^{(x_4)}$ is Hermitian, $B^2 = 1$, with eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$ eight times each: signature (8,8).
\item $S^{ab} = \tfrac14[\gamma^{(a)}, \gamma^{(b)}]$ satisfy the so(4,4) relations; $(S^{ab})^T C + C S^{ab} = 0$, so $S = \bar\Phi\Phi$ is Spin(4,4)-invariant; $[\Gamma, S^{ab}] = 0$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pin(4,4) and Spin(4,4).} The 16-dimensional representation is irreducible under Pin(4,4) (commutant dimension 1: the 256 Clifford products span all of $M_{16}(\mathbb{R})$). Under Spin(4,4) it splits into the two chiral halves $\Gamma = -1$ and $\Gamma = +1$, each irreducible (commutant dimension 1 on each half), and the two 8-dimensional representations are inequivalent (intertwiner dimension 0; the commutant of all $S^{ab}$ on the 16 has dimension 2, spanned by the chiral projectors). Every reflection $\gamma^{(a)}$ exchanges the halves. Pin(4,4) is the double cover of O(4,4), Spin(4,4) of SO(4,4).

\textbf{Consequence used below.} Since $C^2 = 1$ and $C = \mathrm{diag}(-\sigma, \sigma)$ is not $\pm1$, $C$ has both eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$: the bilinear $S = \Phi^\dagger C\Phi$ is an indefinite Hermitian form and takes both signs.

\section{Coupling to the primordial field: vielbein and canonical spin connection}

\textbf{Vielbein.} The diagonal vielbein $e^a{}_\mu = f_a\delta^a_\mu$ with

\[
\begin{aligned}
& f_{x_0} = e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\cot z, \qquad f_{x_1} = f_{x_2} = f_{x_3} = e^{a_4}, \qquad f_{x_4} = 1, \\
& f_{x_5} = f_{x_6} = f_{x_7} = e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}(\sin^2 z)^{1/12}
\end{aligned}
\]

($f_{x_0}$ signed) reproduces the corrected metric entry by entry, $g = e^T\eta e$, with inverse $e_a{}^\mu = \delta_a^\mu/f_a$ and $\sqrt{|g|} = \sigma_z f_{x_0}\cdots f_{x_7}$, $\sigma_z = \mathrm{sign}(\cot z)$, constant between the degenerate points.

\textbf{Levi-Civita connection.} The 21 independent nonzero Christoffel symbols ($m \le n$) are, for $i = x_1, x_2, x_3$ and $t = x_5, x_6, x_7$ (no sum),

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_0 x_0} = -12H\csc(12Hx_0), \qquad \Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_0 x_4} = h_4, \qquad \Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_t x_t} = H(\sin^2 z)^{1/6}\tan z, \\
&\Gamma^{x_i}{}_{x_i x_4} = a_4', \qquad \Gamma^{x_4}{}_{x_i x_i} = e^{2a_4}a_4', \qquad \Gamma^{x_4}{}_{x_0 x_0} = e^{-2a_4}b_4\cot^2 z\;h_4, \\
&\Gamma^{x_4}{}_{x_t x_t} = -e^{-2a_4}b_4(\sin^2 z)^{1/6}\,h_4, \qquad \Gamma^{x_t}{}_{x_t x_0} = H\cot z, \qquad \Gamma^{x_t}{}_{x_t x_4} = h_4 .
\end{aligned}
\]

The hidden direction and the extra times share the rate $h_4$; 3-space is independent of $x_0$.

\textbf{Canonical spin connection.} $\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b = e^a{}_\nu(\partial_\mu e_b{}^\nu + \Gamma^\nu{}_{\mu\lambda}e_b{}^\lambda)$ satisfies the vielbein postulate $\partial_\mu e^a{}_\nu - \Gamma^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}e^a{}_\lambda + \omega_\mu{}^a{}_b e^b{}_\nu = 0$ for all 512 components, and $\omega_{\mu ab} = \eta_{ac}\omega_\mu{}^c{}_b = -\omega_{\mu ba}$. Exactly 10 components with $a < b$ are nonzero ($s_b = e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}$):

\[
\begin{aligned}
& \omega_{x_0,(x_0)(x_4)} = h_4\,s_b\cot z, \qquad \omega_{x_i,(x_i)(x_4)} = a_4'e^{a_4}, \\
& \omega_{x_t,(x_4)(x_t)} = h_4\,s_b(\sin^2 z)^{1/12}, \qquad \omega_{x_t,(x_0)(x_t)} = H(\sin^2 z)^{1/12} ,
\end{aligned}
\]

and $\omega_{x_4} = 0$. Each component is $h_4$, $a_4'$ or $H$ times a factor that does not vanish between the degenerate points.

\textbf{Spinor connection and covariant derivative.} $\Omega_\mu = \tfrac12\omega_{\mu ab}S^{ab}$, a real matrix:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_{x_0} &= \tfrac12 h_4\,s_b\cot z\;\gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_4)}, \qquad \Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac12 a_4'e^{a_4}\,\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_4)}, \qquad i = x_1, x_2, x_3, \\
\Omega_{x_t} &= \tfrac12(\sin^2 z)^{1/12}\,\big(h_4\,s_b\,\gamma^{(x_4)}\gamma^{(x_t)} + H\,\gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_t)}\big), \qquad t = x_5, x_6, x_7, \qquad \Omega_{x_4} = 0,
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
D_\mu\Phi = \partial_\mu\Phi + \Omega_\mu\Phi, \qquad D_\mu\bar\Phi = \partial_\mu\bar\Phi - \bar\Phi\,\Omega_\mu, \qquad \gamma^\mu = e^\mu{}_a\gamma^{(a)} .
\]

This is the canonical connection: it makes the gammas covariantly constant,

\[
D_\mu\gamma^\nu = \partial_\mu\gamma^\nu + \Gamma^\nu{}_{\mu\lambda}\gamma^\lambda + [\Omega_\mu, \gamma^\nu] = 0
\]

for all 64 pairs $(\mu, \nu)$. It is built from the mixed $\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b$ lowered with $\eta$ (SPEC section 3), not from a contraction of the mixed components without the metric factor.

\textbf{Curvature.} $R^\mu{}_\nu$ is diagonal and independent of $x_0$ (unlike the metric of 2026-10-14, there is no off-diagonal Ricci component):

\begin{enumerate}

\[

\section{Non-triviality [2]: theorem and proof}

The author's requirement [2]: the Lagrangian of dirac16complex00 must be non-trivial, i.e. its Euler-Lagrange equations always possess nonzero contributions from gravity through the canonical spin connection unless the spacetime is flat 4+4, and they must be self-consistent, checked and verified.

\textbf{Theorem (NON-TRIVIALITY [2]).} Hypotheses: the corrected metric of section 1 with $H > 0$ and $z$ between degenerate points; $a_4$ and $b_4 > 0$ any differentiable functions of $x_4$ (a constant $a_4$ included; the identities hold for a constant $b_4$ as well, which section 1 excludes as a history); the diagonal vielbein of section 3 and its canonical spin connection; $\Phi$ the commuting 16-component field with the Lagrangian of section 4 and any real potential $U$. Then statements (1) to (4) hold. Statements (1) and (2) concern the diagonal vielbein and the field variables $\Phi$; statements (3) and (4) are the frame-independent content (see "Scope of [2]" below).

\textbf{(1) The gravitational term of the field equation.} The spin-connection part of the Euler-Lagrange operator is

\[
\gamma^\mu D_\mu\Phi - \gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\Phi = \gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu\,\Phi, \qquad \gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu = \tfrac32He^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}\,\gamma^{(x_0)} + \big(u - \tfrac12a_4'\big)\,\gamma^{(x_4)} = c_g\,\gamma^{(x_0)} + v_g\,\gamma^{(x_4)} ,
\]

a matrix independent of $x_0$ for every real $z$, never zero for $H > 0$ (its $\gamma^{(x_0)}$ coefficient $c_g$ is positive). Since $\gamma^{(x_0)}$ and $\gamma^{(x_4)}$ anticommute, with squares $+1$ and $-1$,

\[
\big(\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu\big)^2 = \big(\tfrac94H^2k - (u - \tfrac12a_4')^2\big)\,I_{16} ,
\]

so the term is nonzero for every $\Phi \ne 0$ wherever $c_g^2 \ne v_g^2$; on the background history $c_g = \tfrac32He^{Hx_4/3}$, $v_g = \tfrac56H$ and the square is $H^2\big(\tfrac94e^{2Hx_4/3} - \tfrac{25}{36}\big)I_{16} > 0$; where $c_g = \pm v_g$ the matrix is $c_g(\gamma^{(x_0)} \pm \gamma^{(x_4)})$, of rank 8, and annihilates an 8-dimensional subspace.

\textbf{(2) What each direction contributes.} Per direction (no sum)

\[
\begin{aligned}
\gamma^{x_0}\Omega_{x_0} &= \tfrac{h_4}{2}\,\gamma^{(x_4)}, \qquad \gamma^{x_i}\Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac{a_4'}{2}\,\gamma^{(x_4)} \qquad (i = x_1, x_2, x_3), \\
\gamma^{x_t}\Omega_{x_t} &= \tfrac{h_4}{2}\,\gamma^{(x_4)} + \tfrac{H}{2}e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}\,\gamma^{(x_0)} \qquad (t = x_5, x_6, x_7),
\end{aligned}
\]

and $\gamma^{x_4}\Omega_{x_4} = 0$. The $\gamma^{(x_4)}$ terms add to $\tfrac32a_4' + 4\cdot\tfrac{h_4}{2} = u - \tfrac12a_4'$: half the logarithmic rate of the 7-volume, $\tfrac12\partial_4\ln\sqrt{|g|}$; the hidden-direction ($\gamma^{(x_0)}$) terms of the three extra times add to $3 \cdot \tfrac{H}{2}e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2} = c_g$. Unlike the metric of 2026-10-14, where the $a_4'$ terms cancelled, the term now sees the deflation of the block through $e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}$ and $h_4$.

\textbf{(3) It vanishes in no frame where the metric is curved.} $\Omega_\mu = 0$ for all $\mu$ if and only if $a_4' = 0$, $b_4' = 0$ and $H = 0$; $H = 0$ is a degenerate limit outside the family, so for every admissible metric the spin connection is nonzero in the diagonal frame. Whether a frame can remove it is decided by the curvature: $[D_\mu, D_\nu] = \tfrac12 R_{ab\mu\nu}S^{ab}$, so $\Omega_\mu$ vanishes in no frame wherever the Riemann tensor is nonzero, in particular on the background history; on the flat member $a_4 = 0$, $b_4 = H^2(x_4 - c)^2$ (and only where the metric is flat) it is a pure frame effect.

\textbf{(4) The vielbein in every derivative term.} Gravity also enters every derivative term,

\[
\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu = e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}\tan z\,\gamma^{(x_0)}\partial_0 + e^{-a_4}\gamma^{(x_i)}\partial_i + \gamma^{(x_4)}\partial_4 + e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}(\sin^2 z)^{-1/12}\gamma^{(x_t)}\partial_t

(sums over $i$ and $t$), none of the factors being identically 1.

\[
\gamma^{x_i} = e^{-a_4}\gamma^{(x_i)}, \qquad \Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac12 a_4'e^{a_4}\,\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_4)} .

The scale factors cancel and $(\gamma^{(x_i)})^2 = \eta^{ii} = +1$, so $\gamma^{x_i}\Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac12a_4'\gamma^{(x_4)}$.

Step (b), the extra times $t = x_5, x_6, x_7$:

\gamma^{x_t} = e^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}(\sin^2 z)^{-1/12}\,\gamma^{(x_t)}, \qquad \Omega_{x_t} = \tfrac12(\sin^2 z)^{1/12}\,\big(h_4\,s_b\,\gamma^{(x_4)}\gamma^{(x_t)} + H\gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_t)}\big) .

\section{What is not claimed}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No quantisation.} dirac16complex00 is not quantised; its energy-momentum tensor is a classical bilinear. The operator statements (Krein space, Fock space, normal ordering) belong to dirac16complex.
\item \textbf{The energy is not bounded below, the charge is indefinite.} The record decides the energy question negatively: already for $U = 0$ in the good sector there are positive-frequency solutions of negative energy, and the energy density $-5|c|^2$ of the example of section 9 is unbounded below as $|c|$ grows; the charge $\int\sqrt{|g|}\,\Phi^\dagger B\Phi$ is indefinite and only locally conserved (it is constant only if no charge flows through the degenerate points; see the item on the fold below). The descriptions "semi-classical" and "analogue of Dirac's 1928 wave function" are interpretations: that wave function has a positive conserved density, this field does not.
\item \textbf{Frame dependence of the gravitational term.} $\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu = \tfrac32He^{a_4}b_4^{-1/2}\gamma^{(x_0)} + (u - \tfrac12a_4')\gamma^{(x_4)}$ is the value for the diagonal vielbein and the variables $\Phi$; its $c_g$ part vanishes in the boosted frame of section 6 (no boost in this plane removes its $v_g$ part), and the rescaling $\Phi = e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}(\sin^2 z)^{-1/8}\chi$ removes all of it. Only $\Omega_\mu$ itself where the metric is curved and the frame factors are frame-independent; non-triviality [2] is claimed in this exact sense. On the flat member $a_4 = 0$, $b_4 = H^2(x_4 - c)^2$ the spin connection is a pure frame effect.
\item \textbf{No 3-space observer equation of state, no CPL fit, no comparison with supernova data.} Section 10 gives the ratios $p/\rho$ of the 8-dimensional tensor only; Hypothesis00 (a time-varying dark-energy or dark-matter equation of state from dirac16complex00) is investigated in the separate dark-sector record \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/docs/DA\allowbreak{}RK\_SECTO\allowbreak{}R\_HYPOTH\allowbreak{}ESES}, not here (its verdict is summarised in section 10: it establishes neither hypothesis). For the homogeneous condensate with $\lambda \ne 0$ the ratio $w$ drifts with $a_4$ and $b_4$.
\item \textbf{No general solution, no well-posed Cauchy problem.} The exact solutions of section 5 are test points; the field equation is not solved in general, and no existence statement for the nonlinear Cauchy problem is made beyond the first-order evolution form. For data that depend on the extra times the problem is ill-posed in Hadamard's sense (section 7), and the good sector needs a boundary condition at the degenerate points, which this record does not impose.
\item \textbf{No history from a condensate.} Section 12 shows that a homogeneous condensate is the source of no exponential history in Einstein gravity, that with the author's weights it is a source at most on the constant-$k$ histories named there (not decided further), and that no condensate is the source of the background history; non-exponential histories are not decided, and no non-homogeneous source (which would drive $a_4''$ and $v$ through differences of the kinetic terms) is constructed.
\item \textbf{The $x_0$ dependence.} The corrected metric is an exact solution only with a source whose diagonal components are independent of $x_0$, with $p_0 = p_t$ and without off-diagonal components; a dirac16complex00 configuration whose diagonal components depend on $x_0$ is not a consistent source for this metric.
\item \textbf{The fold.} The points $z = \pi/2 + n\pi$ are degenerate surfaces of the metric ($g_{00} = 0$, $\sqrt{|g|} = 0$); no junction condition is derived, the mirror correspondence used by T2 is an isometry statement and not an orbifold, and the boundary term $\sigma_z b_4^{3/2}(\sin^2 z)^{1/4}u^\dagger M_0 v$ of the hidden direction does not vanish there.
\item \textbf{Pairing.} The pairing theorems of section 13 derive no creation process, rate or amplitude for universes of masses $\{+m, -m\}$; they are correspondences between solutions, in a fixed gravitational field.
\item \textbf{The withdrawn metrics.} The metric of 2026-10-14 (with the factors $\sin^{1/3}z$, $g_{00} = \cot^2 z$ and the patch $0 < z < \pi/2$) and the version 1 of 2026-10-16 are withdrawn; no statement of this document is restricted to them, and their formulas are not results of this record.
\item \textbf{Notebook conventions.} The canonical spin connection used here is the one with $\omega_{\mu ab} = \eta_{ac}\omega_\mu{}^c{}_b$; the notebook's contraction of the mixed components (which lacks a metric factor) is not used, and its Majorana-type $L_g$ serves only as the negative control of section 4.
\end{itemize}
