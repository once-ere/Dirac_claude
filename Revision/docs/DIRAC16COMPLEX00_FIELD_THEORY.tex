This document records, for the author's primordial gravitational field of \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/SPEC.md} section 1, the field theory of dirac16complex00: a field $\Phi$ with 16 complex commuting components that transform as a spinor of Pin(4,4) (a set of 16 scalar fields that transform as a Pin(4,4) spinor), treated as a classical ("semi-classical") field. The document gives the Lagrangian and its coupling to gravity through the vielbein and the canonical spin connection, the exact Euler-Lagrange equations (covariant, explicit in the metric, and as 16 component equations), the non-triviality theorem [2] with its proof and its exact scope (in the diagonal vielbein the gravitational term of the field equation is $\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu = 3H\gamma^{(x_0)} + \tfrac34(b_4'/b_4)\gamma^{(x_4)}$, never zero for $H > 0$; this value depends on the frame and on the field variables, while $\Omega_\mu$ itself vanishes in no frame because the metric is curved), the self-consistency of the equations and its limits (no well-posed Cauchy problem for data that depend on the extra times; boundary terms at $z = \pi/2$), the energy-momentum tensor as a classical bilinear, its kinetic and potential parts, the energy density, the pressures and the equations of state, and the Einstein-Lovelock field equations for $a_4(x_4)$ and $b_4(x_4)$ with dirac16complex00 as the source. Every formula is an output of Revision code (Wolfram and an independent sympy checker for each part); each section ends with the names of the checks that verify it, and the check index collects them. Nothing is taken from the earlier stages of the repository. The classical energy of dirac16complex00 is unbounded below already for $U = 0$ (positive-frequency modes of negative energy) and its charge is indefinite and only locally conserved (the charge of a slice is constant only if no charge flows through the brane $z = \pi/2$, where this record imposes no boundary condition). What the record does not establish is stated in the section "What is not claimed". The verdict of the dark-sector investigation of Hypothesis00 is summarised in section 10, from \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/docs/DA\allowbreak{}RK\_SECTO\allowbreak{}R\_HYPOTH\allowbreak{}ESES}, and section 1 points to the other Revision documents.

\section{The field dirac16complex00 and its symmetry group}

dirac16complex00 is a field $\Phi$ with 16 complex commuting components (16 scalar fields) that transform as a Pin(4,4) spinor. It is a classical field; it is not quantised (SPEC section 6: only dirac16complex, the anticommuting field, is quantised canonically). SPEC section 3 calls it the analogue of Dirac's 1928 wave function and the author calls it semi-classical; both are interpretations, and the record shows two exact differences from Dirac's wave function: its charge $Q = \int b_4^{3/2}\cos z\,\Phi^\dagger B\Phi\,d^7x$ (locally conserved, section 7) is an indefinite form, and its classical energy is unbounded below already for $U = 0$ in the good sector (section 9).

\textbf{The gammas.} The real $16\times16$ matrices are the author's T16, rebuilt in Revision code from the author's formulas (the tau matrices and their blocks), with the frame map $\gamma^{(x_k)} = T16[k]$ for $k = 0, \dots, 7$ ($\gamma^{(x_0)}$ of the hidden direction, $\gamma^{(x_4)}$ of the time). Verified exactly:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\{\gamma^{(a)}, \gamma^{(b)}\} = 2\eta^{ab}$ with $\eta = \mathrm{diag}(+1,+1,+1,+1,-1,-1,-1,-1)$ in the order $x_0 \ldots x_7$; every $\gamma^{(a)}$ is a real signed permutation matrix; the space-like ones are symmetric, the time-like ones antisymmetric.
\item $C = \gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_1)}\gamma^{(x_2)}\gamma^{(x_3)}$ is real symmetric, $C^2 = 1$, and $C\gamma^{(a)}$ is real antisymmetric for every $a$; $C = \mathrm{diag}(-\sigma, \sigma)$ with the notebook's $\sigma$.
\item $\Gamma = \gamma^{(x_0)}\gamma^{(x_1)}\cdots\gamma^{(x_7)} = \mathrm{diag}(-I_8, I_8)$, $\Gamma^2 = 1$, and $\Gamma$ anticommutes with every $\gamma^{(a)}$.
\item $B = -iC\gamma^{(x_4)}$ is Hermitian, $B^2 = 1$, with eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$ eight times each: signature (8,8).
\item $S^{ab} = \tfrac14[\gamma^{(a)}, \gamma^{(b)}]$ satisfy the so(4,4) relations; $(S^{ab})^T C + C S^{ab} = 0$, so $S = \bar\Phi\Phi$ is Spin(4,4)-invariant; $[\Gamma, S^{ab}] = 0$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pin(4,4) and Spin(4,4).} The 16-dimensional representation is irreducible under Pin(4,4) (commutant dimension 1: the 256 Clifford products span all of $M_{16}(\mathbb{R})$). Under Spin(4,4) it splits into the two chiral halves $\Gamma = -1$ and $\Gamma = +1$, each irreducible (commutant dimension 1 on each half), and the two 8-dimensional representations are inequivalent (intertwiner dimension 0; the commutant of all $S^{ab}$ on the 16 has dimension 2, spanned by the chiral projectors). Every reflection $\gamma^{(a)}$ exchanges the halves. Pin(4,4) is the double cover of O(4,4), Spin(4,4) of SO(4,4).

\textbf{Consequence used below.} Since $C^2 = 1$ and $C = \mathrm{diag}(-\sigma, \sigma)$ is not $\pm1$, $C$ has both eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$: the bilinear $S = \Phi^\dagger C\Phi$ is an indefinite Hermitian form and takes both signs.

\section{Coupling to the primordial field: vielbein and canonical spin connection}

\textbf{Vielbein.} The diagonal vielbein $e^a{}_\mu = f_a\delta^a_\mu$ with

\[
\begin{aligned}
& f_{x_0} = \cot z, \qquad f_{x_1} = f_{x_2} = f_{x_3} = e^{a_4}\sin^{1/6}z, \qquad f_{x_4} = 1, \\
& f_{x_5} = f_{x_6} = f_{x_7} = e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\sin^{1/6}z
\end{aligned}
\]

reproduces the author's metric entry by entry, $g = e^T\eta e$, with inverse $e_a{}^\mu = \delta_a^\mu/f_a$ and $\sqrt{|g|} = f_{x_0}\cdots f_{x_7} = b_4^{3/2}\cos z$.

\textbf{Levi-Civita connection.} The 25 independent nonzero Christoffel symbols ($m \le n$) are, for $i = x_1, x_2, x_3$ and $t = x_5, x_6, x_7$ (no sum),

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Gamma^{x_i}{}_{x_i x_4} = a_4', \qquad \Gamma^{x_i}{}_{x_i x_0} = H\cot z, \qquad \Gamma^{x_4}{}_{x_i x_i} = e^{2a_4}\sin^{1/3}z\; a_4', \\
&\Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_i x_i} = -e^{2a_4} H\sec z\,\sin^{4/3}z, \\
&\Gamma^{x_t}{}_{x_4 x_t} = -\big(a_4' - \tfrac12u\big), \qquad \Gamma^{x_t}{}_{x_t x_0} = H\cot z, \qquad \Gamma^{x_4}{}_{x_t x_t} = e^{-2a_4}b_4\sin^{1/3}z\,\big(a_4' - \tfrac12u\big), \\
&\Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_t x_t} = e^{-2a_4}b_4 H\sec z\,\sin^{4/3}z, \qquad \Gamma^{x_0}{}_{x_0 x_0} = -12H\csc(12Hx_0) .
\end{aligned}
\]

\textbf{Canonical spin connection.} $\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b = e^a{}_\nu(\partial_\mu e_b{}^\nu + \Gamma^\nu{}_{\mu\lambda}e_b{}^\lambda)$ satisfies the vielbein postulate $\partial_\mu e^a{}_\nu - \Gamma^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}e^a{}_\lambda + \omega_\mu{}^a{}_b e^b{}_\nu = 0$ for all 512 components, and $\omega_{\mu ab} = \eta_{ac}\omega_\mu{}^c{}_b = -\omega_{\mu ba}$. Exactly 12 components with $a < b$ are nonzero:

\[
\begin{aligned}
& \omega_{x_i,(x_i)(x_4)} = a_4' e^{a_4}\sin^{1/6}z, \qquad \omega_{x_i,(x_i)(x_0)} = H e^{a_4}\sin^{1/6}z, \\
& \omega_{x_t,(x_4)(x_t)} = -\big(a_4' - \tfrac12u\big) e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\sin^{1/6}z, \qquad \omega_{x_t,(x_t)(x_0)} = -H e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\sin^{1/6}z ,
\end{aligned}
\]

and $\omega_{x_0} = \omega_{x_4} = 0$. Each component is $a_4'$, $a_4' - \tfrac12u = -\frac{d}{dx_4}\ln\big(e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\big)$ or $H$ times a factor that never vanishes on the patch.

\textbf{Spinor connection and covariant derivative.} $\Omega_\mu = \tfrac12\omega_{\mu ab}S^{ab}$, a real matrix:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_{x_i} &= \tfrac12 e^{a_4}\sin^{1/6}z\,\big(a_4'\,\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_4)} + H\,\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_0)}\big), \qquad i = x_1, x_2, x_3, \\
\Omega_{x_t} &= -\tfrac12 e^{-a_4}b_4^{1/2}\sin^{1/6}z\,\big(\big(a_4' - \tfrac12u\big)\gamma^{(x_4)}\gamma^{(x_t)} + H\,\gamma^{(x_t)}\gamma^{(x_0)}\big), \qquad t = x_5, x_6, x_7, \\
\Omega_{x_0} &= \Omega_{x_4} = 0,
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
D_\mu\Phi = \partial_\mu\Phi + \Omega_\mu\Phi, \qquad D_\mu\bar\Phi = \partial_\mu\bar\Phi - \bar\Phi\,\Omega_\mu, \qquad \gamma^\mu = e^\mu{}_a\gamma^{(a)} .
\]

This is the canonical connection: it makes the gammas covariantly constant,

\[
D_\mu\gamma^\nu = \partial_\mu\gamma^\nu + \Gamma^\nu{}_{\mu\lambda}\gamma^\lambda + [\Omega_\mu, \gamma^\nu] = 0
\]

for all 64 pairs $(\mu, \nu)$. It is built from the mixed $\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b$ lowered with $\eta$ (SPEC section 3), not from a contraction of the mixed components without the metric factor.

\textbf{Curvature.} $R^\mu{}_\nu$ is diagonal except for the pair $x_4$-$x_0$, which is proportional to $b_4'$:

\[

& R^{x_t}{}_{x_t} = -a_4'' - 6H^2 - \tfrac32a_4'u + \tfrac34u^2 + \tfrac12v, \qquad R^{x_4}{}_{x_0} = \tfrac32Hu\cot z, \qquad R^{x_0}{}_{x_4} = -\tfrac32Hu\tan z,
\end{aligned}

\end{aligned}

\textbf{(3) It vanishes only in flat 4+4 space.} $\Omega_\mu = 0$ for all $\mu$ if and only if $a_4' = 0$, $b_4' = 0$ and $H = 0$; $H = 0$ is a degenerate limit outside the family, so for every admissible metric the spin connection is nonzero. (sums over $i$ and $t$), none of the factors being identically 1; $a_4$ enters the field equation only through these factors (its spin-connection terms cancel by (2)), and $b_4$ through $b_4^{-1/2}$ and through $\tfrac34u\,\gamma^{(x_4)}$.

\textbf{Proof.} Step (a), the inflating directions $i = x_1, x_2, x_3$: by section 3

\[
\gamma^{x_i} = e^{-a_4}\sin^{-1/6}z\,\gamma^{(x_i)}, \qquad \Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac12 e^{a_4}\sin^{1/6}z\,\big(a_4'\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_4)} + H\gamma^{(x_i)}\gamma^{(x_0)}\big) .
\]

The scale factors cancel and $(\gamma^{(x_i)})^2 = \eta^{ii} = +1$, so $\gamma^{x_i}\Omega_{x_i} = \tfrac12(a_4'\gamma^{(x_4)} + H\gamma^{(x_0)})$.

Step (b), the deflating extra times $t = x_5, x_6, x_7$:

The square of statement (1) follows from $\{\gamma^{(x_0)}, \gamma^{(x_4)}\} = 0$.

\section{What is not claimed}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No quantisation.} dirac16complex00 is not quantised; its energy-momentum tensor is a classical bilinear. The operator statements (Krein space, Fock space, normal ordering) belong to dirac16complex.
\item \textbf{The energy is not bounded below, the charge is indefinite.} The record decides the energy question negatively: already for $U = 0$ in the good sector there are positive-frequency solutions of negative energy, and the energy density $-5|c|^2$ of the example of section 9 is unbounded below as $|c|$ grows; the charge $\int b_4^{3/2}\cos z\,\Phi^\dagger B\Phi$ is indefinite and only locally conserved (it is constant only if no charge flows through the brane $z = \pi/2$; see the item on the brane below). The descriptions "semi-classical" and "analogue of Dirac's 1928 wave function" are interpretations: that wave function has a positive conserved density, this field does not.
\item \textbf{Frame dependence of the gravitational term.} $\gamma^\mu\Omega_\mu = 3H\gamma^{(x_0)} + \tfrac34u\,\gamma^{(x_4)}$ is the value for the diagonal vielbein and the variables $\Phi$; its $H$ part vanishes in the boosted frame of section 6 (no boost in this plane removes its $b_4'$ part), and the rescaling $\Phi = b_4^{-3/4}\sin^{-1/2}z\,\chi$ removes all of it. Only $\Omega_\mu$ itself (curvature) and the frame factors are frame-independent; non-triviality [2] is claimed in this exact sense.
\item \textbf{No 3-space observer equation of state, no CPL fit, no comparison with supernova data.} Section 10 gives the ratios $p/\rho$ of the 8-dimensional tensor only; Hypothesis00 (a time-varying dark-energy or dark-matter equation of state from dirac16complex00) is investigated in the separate dark-sector record \texttt{Revision\allowbreak{}/docs/DA\allowbreak{}RK\_SECTO\allowbreak{}R\_HYPOTH\allowbreak{}ESES}, not here (its verdict is summarised in section 10: it establishes neither hypothesis). For the homogeneous condensate with $\lambda \ne 0$ the ratio $w$ drifts with $b_4$.
\item \textbf{No general solution, no well-posed Cauchy problem.} The exact solutions of section 5 are test points; the field equation is not solved in general, and no existence statement for the nonlinear Cauchy problem is made beyond the first-order evolution form. For data that depend on the extra times the problem is ill-posed in Hadamard's sense (section 7), and the good sector needs a boundary condition at $z = \pi/2$, which this record does not impose.
\item \textbf{No history from a condensate.} Section 12 shows that a homogeneous condensate cannot be the source of the metric with $b_4$ not constant in Einstein gravity, that with the author's weights it is the source of no exponential history with $b_4$ not constant, and that no condensate is the source of the background history; non-exponential histories are not decided, the sign of $A$ is not selected by the equations, and no non-homogeneous source (which would drive $a_4''$ and $v$ through differences of the kinetic terms) is constructed.
\item \textbf{The $x_0$ dependence.} The metric of section 1 is an exact solution only with an isotropic source whose diagonal components are independent of $x_0$ and whose off-diagonal components vanish except the pair $x_4$-$x_0$ of section 11, which must be $\cot z\,u\,Q/\kappa$; a dirac16complex00 configuration whose diagonal components depend on $x_0$ is not a consistent source for this metric.
\item \textbf{The brane.} The patch end $z = \pi/2$ is a degenerate surface of the metric ($g_{00} = 0$, $\sqrt{|g|} = 0$) at finite proper distance; no junction condition is derived, the mirror construction used by T2 is an assumption, and the boundary term $b_4^{3/2}\sin z\,u^\dagger M_0 v$ of the hidden direction does not vanish there.
\item \textbf{Pairing.} The pairing theorems of section 13 derive no creation process, rate or amplitude for universes of masses $\{+m, -m\}$; they are correspondences between solutions, in a fixed gravitational field.
\item \textbf{The withdrawn metric.} The earlier metric with $b_4 = 1$ is withdrawn; no statement of this document is restricted to it, and its formulas are not results of this record (they are the values of the formulas above at $u = v = 0$, a case the record excludes).
\item \textbf{Notebook conventions.} The canonical spin connection used here is the one with $\omega_{\mu ab} = \eta_{ac}\omega_\mu{}^c{}_b$; the notebook's contraction of the mixed components (which lacks a metric factor) is not used, and its Majorana-type $L_g$ serves only as the negative control of section 4.
\end{itemize}
