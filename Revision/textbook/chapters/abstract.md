This book teaches, starting from school algebra and the calculus of one variable, the complete theory of two fields in the author's primordial universe, and every computation that the Revision record of the repository has made about them. The universe has eight directions, named as the author names them: three directions of ordinary space, $x_1$, $x_2$ and $x_3$, which inflate; the time $x_4$; three extra times, $x_5$, $x_6$ and $x_7$, which deflate exponentially; and a hidden space direction $x_8$. Four of the directions are space-like and four are time-like (signature (4,4)). The fields are dirac16complex, a spinor field with sixteen complex anticommuting (Grassmann) components, and dirac16complex00, its version with sixteen commuting components.

Part I builds the mathematics and the geometry of the author's metric. Part II builds the Clifford algebra Cl(4,4), the facts about Pin(4,4) and Spin(4,4), the charge-conjugation matrices, the Lagrangians, the field equations, the energy-momentum tensors and the canonical quantisation of the two fields. Part III builds the generalized Kronecker delta, the Lovelock tensors and the field equations for the metric function $a_4$. Part IV introduces density functional theory from zero and solves the Kohn-Sham model of dirac16complex in the deflating field with a Rust program, checked by an independent solver; the Kohn-Sham history of $a_4$ is a prescribed background, not a solution of the field equations for $a_4$. Part V proves the pairing theorems and examines pair creation and matter and antimatter.

The book proves exact maps between the solutions of mass $+m$ and those of mass $-m$ (the pairing theorems T1, T2, Q and T3). It does not prove that the big bang creates universes, in pairs or otherwise: no creation process, rate or amplitude follows from these equations. It proves that the charge of the theory obeys an exact local conservation law (inside the patch $0 < z < \pi/2$ of the hidden direction charge can only move: no process creates or destroys it at any point there), shows that the total charge of a universe is constant only if no charge flows through the edge $z = \pi/2$ of the hidden direction (a condition that is assumed, not derived), proves that a pair of partner solutions has total charge zero, and shows that the theory as built does not explain the excess of matter over antimatter. Every statement carries one of the labels PROVED, COMPUTED, ASSUMED, HYPOTHESIS or OPEN. Every worked example is a complete Jupyter notebook, printed in full together with complete instructions for running it on Windows, macOS and Linux, its figures and a line-by-line walk-through; every number in the text is computed by a notebook or taken from a Revision record.
