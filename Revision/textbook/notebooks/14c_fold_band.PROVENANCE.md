Right: the numerical slope for six pairs of mass $M$ and cutoff $L$ (units of $m$ and $1/H$) at the slices 0 (left bars) and 1 (right bars): every value is $1$ or $e^{-1}$ (dotted lines), whatever $M$ and $L$, because the band orbital $(e^{fMy}, 0)$ has $\chi^\dagger\sigma_3\chi = \chi^\dagger\chi$ and $\kappa$ is the same at every $y$.
