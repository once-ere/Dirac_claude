### 3.4 Figures

The notebook shows 7 figures, each below the cell that draws it, and saves each as a PNG file (150 dots per inch, no metadata):

- `Revision/textbook/figures/09b_1_sign_of_s.png` (939 x 620 pixels): Histogram of $S_0 = \chi^\dagger C\chi$ for 20000 random complex columns $\chi$ of length 1 (fixed seed); horizontal axis $S_0$ (a pure number between $-1$ and $1$, because the eigenvalues of $C$ are $\pm 1$), vertical axis the number of $\chi$ in each of 60 intervals. The values are spread symmetrically about $0$: about half are negative. For a condensate with $\lambda = 0$ the energy density is $\rho = mS = mS_0e^{a_4}b_4^{-2}$, so its sign is the sign of $S_0$.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09b_2_condensate_solutions.png` (1253 x 576 pixels): Two condensates $\Phi = e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}\chi$ of dirac16complex00 with $m = 1$, $H = 0.25$ and $S_0 = 1$ along the history $a_4 = AHx_4$, $b_4 = e^{4AHx_4/3}$ ($A = 1$), for $x_4$ from $0$ to $16$; the gravitational coefficient $c_g = 0.375\,e^{x_4/12}$ grows from 0.375 to 1.42. Left, $\lambda = 0.5$: the effective mass $m + \lambda S$ falls from 1.5 towards 1; the real parts of the components 1, 6 and 11 of $\Phi$ oscillate and shrink, and $S$ (dashed) falls like $e^{-5x_4/12}$; after $x_4 = 11.81$ (dotted), where $c_g$ passes the effective mass, the components grow. Right, $\lambda = -0.8$, logarithmic axis: the size $\chi^\dagger\chi$ of the column and the size $\Phi^\dagger\Phi$ of the field (dashed); the condensate grows until $x_4 = 0.68$, oscillates until $x_4 = 11.70$ (dotted lines) and grows again. All values are pure numbers.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09b_3_energy_and_pressure.png` (918 x 615 pixels): Energy density $\rho = mS + \lambda S^2/2$ (solid), pressure $p = \lambda S^2/2$ (solid) and the two parts of the pressure, the kinetic part $(m + \lambda S)S$ (dashed) and the potential part $-(mS + U)$ (dotted), of a condensate of dirac16complex00 with $m = 1$, $\lambda = 0.5$, against $S$ from $-6$ to $2$; vertical axis in units of energy per unit volume. The energy density vanishes at $S = 0$ and $S = -2m/\lambda = -4$ (black dots) and is negative between them; the pressure is the same in all seven directions.
