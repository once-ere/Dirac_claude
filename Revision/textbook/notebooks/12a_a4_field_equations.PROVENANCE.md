## 1. What the notebook computes

It derives with sympy, from the corrected metric of the author with its two functions a4(x4) and b4(x4), for every real value of the hidden angle, the Christoffel symbols, the Riemann, Ricci and Einstein tensors and the three Lovelock tensors (with the generalized Kronecker delta), writes the Einstein-Lovelock field equations, reduces them to a constraint, two equations of second order and the conditions on the source, checks the Bianchi identity and the conservation law of a general source, specialises to Einstein gravity (a flat vacuum, no exponential vacuum, the null energy combinations) and to the exponential histories with the source of the record history, compares every result with the Revision records, and draws five teaching plots.

It reads or reproduces these Revision records:

- `Revision/field_equations_a4/reports/python-a4-report.json`: the sympy checks of the a4 and b4 record that the notebook reproduces
- `Revision/field_equations_a4/reports/wolfram-a4-report.json`: the Wolfram checks of the a4 and b4 record that the notebook reproduces
