## 1. What the notebook computes

It integrates the evolution equation of the author's metric, in which the second derivative of a4 times the factor F equals kappa (p3 - p_t), with the fourth-order Runge-Kutta method for prescribed anisotropic stresses p3 - p_t (zero, a pulse that raises the deflation rate, a stress that relaxes it, a constant stress in Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity), with the extra times deflating along every history, compares every history a4(x4) with its exact solution, measures the convergence order, computes the energy density and the pressures that each history requires, checks numerically that the constraint and the conservation law hold along the solutions, and draws six teaching plots of a4, its rate and the scale factors of 3-space and of the deflating extra times.
