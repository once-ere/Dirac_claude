## 1. What the notebook computes

It integrates the two equations of second order for a4 and b4 of the corrected metric, which determine the second derivatives of a4 and of ln b4 from the 3-space pressure and the hidden pressure, with the fourth-order Runge-Kutta method for prescribed sources: no source (the flat vacuum), the source of the record history (in Einstein gravity and with the author's weights), the same source from other starting rates, the source of a history whose rate rises in a pulse, and a gas of massless particles in 3-space with the author's weights, where the system becomes singular. It compares every history with its exact solution, measures the convergence order, checks that the energy density integrated from the conservation law equals the one the constraint gives, and draws seven teaching plots. At the end it reads the record on the Kohn-Sham states and recomputes its numbers from the table of their integrated energy-momentum tensors.

It reads or reproduces these Revision records:

- `Revision/field_equations_a4/a4-equations.json`: the Lovelock components and the source of the record history
- `Revision/field_equations_a4/reports/python-a4-report.json`: the sympy checks of the a4 and b4 record that the notebook reproduces
- `Revision/field_equations_a4/reports/wolfram-a4-report.json`: the Wolfram checks of the a4 and b4 record that the notebook reproduces
- `Revision/field_equations_a4/ks_source/reports/ks-source-a4.json`: the record of the gas of massless particles as a source, whose singular point the notebook reproduces
- `Revision/field_equations_a4/reports/ks-source-conditions.json`: the record that no Kohn-Sham state of the repository is an admissible source
- `Revision/kohn_sham/results/ground/emt-integrals.csv`: the integrals over the hidden direction of the energy density and the pressures of every recorded Kohn-Sham ground state, from which the notebook recomputes the numbers of the record ks-source-conditions.json
