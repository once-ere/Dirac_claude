### 3.4 Figures

The notebook shows 4 figures, each below the cell that draws it, and saves each as a PNG file (150 dots per inch, no metadata):

- `Revision/textbook/figures/00b_1_eta_heat_map.png` (983 x 720 pixels): The signs of the author's eight directions drawn as a heat map: the 8 by 8 diagonal matrix $\eta$ with the sign of each coordinate on its diagonal and 0 everywhere else; rows and columns are labelled by the coordinates $x_1$ to $x_8$, and the entries are pure numbers. Red squares are $+1$ (space-like: $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$, $x_8$), blue squares $-1$ (time-like: the time $x_4$ and the extra times $x_5$, $x_6$, $x_7$), grey squares 0. Four entries are $+1$ and four are $-1$: the signature is (4,4).
- `Revision/textbook/figures/00b_2_scale_factors.png` (957 x 611 pixels): The length factors of the author's metric at a fixed hidden coordinate, divided by $\sin^{1/6} z$, against the metric function $a_4$ from 0 to 3 (horizontal axis, a pure number; vertical axis logarithmic, a pure number). Solid blue: $e^{a_4}$, the factor of each direction of ordinary space, which grows to $e^3 = 20.09$; dashed orange: $e^{-a_4}$, the factor of each extra time, which shrinks to $e^{-3} = 0.0498$; dotted black: the product of the three space factors and the three extra-time factors, which stays exactly 1. On the logarithmic axis both exponentials are straight lines.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/00b_3_length_factors.png` (928 x 611 pixels): The eight length factors $f_i = \sqrt{|g_{ii}|}$ of the author's metric at the hidden coordinate $z = \pi/4$ for $a_4 = 0$ (blue), $a_4 = 0.5$ (orange) and $a_4 = 1$ (aqua); horizontal axis the directions $x_1$ to $x_8$, vertical axis the factor (a pure number). The factors of ordinary space $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$ grow from 0.94 to 2.57 as $a_4$ grows from 0 to 1, those of the extra times $x_5$, $x_6$, $x_7$ shrink from 0.94 to 0.35, and those of the time $x_4$ and of the hidden direction $x_8$ stay 1. The product of the eight factors is $\cos(\pi/4) = 0.7071$ in all three cases.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/00b_4_volume_factor.png` (928 x 611 pixels): The volume factor $\sqrt{|\det g|} = f_1 f_2 \cdots f_8$ of the author's metric against the hidden coordinate $z = 6 H x_8$ from 0 to $\pi/2$ (horizontal axis, in radians; vertical axis a pure number), computed from the eight entries for $a_4 = 0$ (blue circles), $a_4 = 1$ (orange squares) and $a_4 = 2$ (aqua triangles), with the curve $\cos z$ (black line). All three sets of points lie on the curve: the volume factor does not depend on $a_4$, because the inflation of ordinary space and the deflation of the extra times cancel; it falls from 1 at $z = 0$ to 0 at $z = \pi/2$.
