### 3.4 Figures

The notebook shows 8 figures, each below the cell that draws it, and saves each as a PNG file (150 dots per inch, no metadata):

- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_1_einstein_source.png` (935 x 611 pixels): The source that the record history $a_4 = Hx_4$, $b_4 = e^{(4/3)Hx_4}$ requires in Einstein gravity with $\Lambda = 0$, as a function of the time $x_4$ (units $1/H$), in units of $H^2/\kappa$: the energy density $\kappa\rho = -6k - 1/3$ (solid), the 3-space pressure $\kappa p_3 = 6k - 13/9$ (dashed) and the common pressure of the hidden direction and the extra times $\kappa p_0 = \kappa p_t = 3k - 11/3$ (dotted), with $k = e^{2x_4/3}$. All three grow with $k$; the energy density is negative at every time, and $p_3 \neq p_0$: the source is anisotropic.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_2_sign_symmetry.png` (1400 x 615 pixels): Einstein gravity, $\beta = 4/3$, $\Lambda = 0$: the energy density $\kappa\rho$ that the exponential histories with slope $A = 1$ (solid) and $A = -1$ (dashed) require, in units of $H^2$. Left, as a function of $k = e^{2a_4}/b_4$: the two curves coincide, because the source contains $A$ only through $A^2$. Right, as a function of the time $x_4$ (units $1/H$): the curves are mirror images under $x_4 \to -x_4$, because $k = e^{(2/3)Ax_4}$ depends only on the product $Ax_4$. Whether 3-space inflates or deflates is an initial condition, not selected by the equations.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_3_beta_scan.png` (935 x 616 pixels): Einstein gravity with $A = 1$ and $\Lambda = 0$: the source that an exponential history requires at a moment where $k = 1$, as a function of $\beta = u/a_4'$ from 0 to 4, in units of $H^2/\kappa$: the energy density $\kappa\rho = 3(\beta^2 - 2)/2 - 6$ (solid), the 3-space pressure (dashed) and the common pressure of the hidden direction and the extra times (dotted). The dotted vertical line is the record value $\beta = 4/3$, the dashed one $\beta = 2$, where the block of the hidden direction and the extra times does not shrink and the source is constant in time. The energy density is positive only for $\beta > \sqrt{6}$.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_4_author_weights_source.png` (935 x 611 pixels): The source that the record history $a_4 = Hx_4$, $b_4 = e^{(4/3)Hx_4}$ requires with the author's weights $\alpha_k = w_k/H^{2k}$, $w = (1, 1/32, 1/1152)$, and $\Lambda = -10$, as a function of the time $x_4$ (units $1/H$, $H = \kappa = 1$): the energy density $\kappa\rho$ (solid), the 3-space pressure $\kappa p_3$ (dashed) and the common pressure $\kappa p_0 = \kappa p_t$ (dotted). The energy density starts at $3.57407$ and changes sign at $x_4 = 0.78$ (grey line); the black dots are its values at the slices $a_4 = 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2$ printed by the record of the source conditions. A source whose energy density changes sign is not ordinary matter.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_5_null_energy_map.png` (928 x 611 pixels): Einstein gravity on the exponential histories: the plane of $\beta$ (horizontal) and $k/A^2$ (vertical, $k = e^{2a_4}/b_4$). Blue: the null energy condition along $x_4 + x_1$, $\rho + p_3 \geq 0$, holds ($2 \leq \beta \leq 4$). Pink: the null energy condition along $x_4 + x_0$, $\rho + p_0 \geq 0$, holds (below the black line $k/A^2 = (\beta - 4)/2$). Dark red: both hold, only where $k < 0$. Every history lies above the horizontal line $k = 0$, where the two regions do not overlap: no exponential history has a source that satisfies both conditions. The dotted line is the record history $\beta = 4/3$, where both are violated.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/12b_6_gauss_bonnet_vacuum.png` (1387 x 646 pixels): The vacuum solutions of Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity ($\alpha_1 = 1$, $\alpha_3 = 0$) among the exponential histories: they exist only for constant $k = k_0$ ($\beta = 2$), here with $A = 1$. Left: the coupling $\alpha_2 H^2 = -1/(4(A^2 - k_0))$; right: the cosmological constant $\Lambda/H^2 = 3(A^4 + 3A^2k_0 + k_0^2)/(A^2 - k_0)$, both against $k_0/A^2$.
