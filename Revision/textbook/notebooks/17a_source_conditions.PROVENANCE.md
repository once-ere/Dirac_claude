## 1. What the notebook computes

It derives with sympy, from the Lovelock components stored in the Revision record of the a4 field equations, the three conditions that every source of the author's metric must meet (C1: no dependence on the hidden coordinate x8; C2: p3 + p_t = 2 p8; C3: for the linear member a4 = A H x4, equal pressures and a constant energy density). It then evaluates the three conditions on the 75 committed Kohn-Sham ground states of dirac16complex, reproduces every number of the five checks of the Revision record ks-source-conditions.json, shows that no recorded Kohn-Sham state is an admissible source, so that the Kohn-Sham history is a prescribed background, and draws seven teaching plots.
