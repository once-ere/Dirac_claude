### 3.4 Figures

The notebook shows 5 figures, each below the cell that draws it, and saves each as a PNG file (150 dots per inch, no metadata):

- `Revision/textbook/figures/09c_1_generic_condensate.png` (876 x 741 pixels): Heat map of the 64 entries $T^\nu{}_\mu$ (row $\nu$, column $\mu$, both running over $x_0, \dots, x_7$) of a generic exact condensate of dirac16complex00 with effective mass $V = 5$, $\lambda = 0$, $H = 1$, a random unit column, at the point $a_4 = 0.5$, $a_4' = 0.25$, $b_4 = e^{2/3}$, $b_4'/b_4 = 1/3$, $z = \pi/4$ of the history in the corrected metric; colour scale from $-2$ to $2$ (entries beyond it are shown in the darkest colour and printed), in units of energy per unit volume. The diagonal holds only $-VS$ at $x_4$; 36 off-diagonal squares are nonzero, in the rows and columns pairing 3-space with $x_0$, with $x_4$ and with the extra times, and $x_4$ with the extra times. The squares $(x_4, x_0)$ and $(x_0, x_4)$ and those inside 3-space and inside the extra times are zero.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09c_2_offdiagonal_entries.png` (1240 x 582 pixels): Four off-diagonal entries of the generic condensate of the previous figure ($V = 5$, $H = 1$, $a_4 = 0.5$, $b_4 = e^{2/3}$, $b_4'/b_4 = 1/3$, $z = \pi/4$) against the rate $a_4'$ from $-1$ to $1$, in units of energy per unit volume. Left: the momentum flows $T^{x_4}{}_{x_1}$ and $T^{x_4}{}_{x_7}$ come from $H$ and do not depend on $a_4'$. Right: $T^{x_1}{}_{x_0}$ (solid) is proportional to $4a_4' - b_4'/b_4$ and vanishes at $a_4' = 1/12$ (dotted line), where 3-space and the block change at the same rate; $T^{x_1}{}_{x_7}$ (dashed) is a part from $H$ plus a part proportional to $4a_4' - b_4'/b_4$, a straight line that keeps its $H$ part at $a_4' = 1/12$.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09c_3_witness_condensate.png` (876 x 741 pixels): Heat map of the 64 entries $T^\nu{}_\mu$ of the exact witness condensate $\Phi = e^{a_4/2}b_4^{-1}e^{-4ix_4}\Phi_0$ of the Revision record with $V = 5$, $H = 1$, $\lambda = 0$, on a history with $k = e^{2a_4}/b_4 = 4$, at the point $a_4 = 0.5$, $a_4' = 0.25$, $b_4 = e/4$, $b_4'/b_4 = 0.5$, $z = \pi/4$ (jet $\Phi_0$), with the same colour scale as the generic condensate, in units of energy per unit volume. All 24 three-gamma bilinears of $\Phi_0$ vanish, so every off-diagonal entry is zero (by the record, at every point of every history with constant $k$): this condensate is a perfect fluid at rest, with $T^{x_4}{}_{x_4} = -VS$ and zero pressure.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09c_4_conservation_convergence.png` (939 x 615 pixels): The largest of the eight components of the covariant divergence $\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_\nu$ of the full tensor of a condensate ($m = 1$, $\lambda = 0.5$, $H = 0.25$, history $a_4 = 0.3x_4 + 0.05x_4^2$, $b_4 = e^{0.4x_4 - 0.02x_4^2}$, point $x_4 = 1$, $z = 0.5$, corrected metric), computed with central finite differences of step $h$, against $h$ from $0.2$ to $0.0002$; both axes logarithmic, the divergence in units of energy per unit volume per unit length. For the true condensate (circles) it falls like $h^2$ (dotted line): the exact divergence is zero. For the control (squares), which leaves out the gravitational part $c_g\gamma^{(4)}\gamma^{(0)}$ of the condensate equation, it stays near $0.044$: its tensor is not conserved.
- `Revision/textbook/figures/09c_5_divergence_components.png` (949 x 577 pixels): The eight components $|\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_\nu|$, $\nu = x_0, \dots, x_7$, of the divergence of the full tensor of the condensate of the previous figure, with finite differences of step $h = 0.001$, on a logarithmic axis (components that vanish exactly are drawn at $10^{-16}$). The true condensate (left bars) has every component at the level of the finite-difference error, below $10^{-6}$. The control (right bars) has its three momentum components along 3-space, $\nu = x_1, x_2, x_3$, between about $0.019$ and $0.044$, while its components $\nu = x_0$, $x_4$ and $x_5, x_6, x_7$ stay at the error level of the true condensate: the control keeps the energy balance, the hidden balance and the extra-time balances but breaks the momentum balance along 3-space.
