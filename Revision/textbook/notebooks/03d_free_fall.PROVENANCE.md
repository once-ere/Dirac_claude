## 1. What the notebook computes

It integrates the geodesic equations of free fall in the author's metric with the Christoffel symbols of the Revision record, along the deflating history a4 = A H x4 with A = 1 and H = 1, by the Runge-Kutta method RK4. With sympy it proves exactly that the six momenta along x1, x2, x3, x5, x6, x7 and the squared length of the velocity are conserved, that the frame velocity in 3-space falls like e^(-a4) (redshift) while the frame velocity along an extra time grows like e^(a4) (blueshift), that the hidden coordinate y is pushed with the acceleration H times the difference of the squared 3-space and extra-time frame velocities, and that the x4 velocity can only decrease; it reproduces the momentum weight of the Revision Kohn-Sham record and the quadratic form of the plane-wave check of the Revision scope report. It checks all of this on the computed paths, finds the turning point in x4 of a particle that moves along an extra time, measures the order 4 of RK4, and draws 5 figures.

It reads or reproduces these Revision records:

- `Revision/gkd_lovelock/results/curvature.json`: the 25 non-zero Christoffel symbols with b <= c (christoffelNonzero_b_le_c), written by the Rust program lovelock_gkd; the notebook integrates the geodesic equations with exactly these symbols
- `Revision/kohn_sham/results/parameters.json`: H = 1 and the slope A = 1 of the deflating history a4 = A H x4 of the Revision Kohn-Sham work (physics.H, physics.historyA)
- `Revision/kohn_sham/ks-theory.json`: the momentum weight kappa(y, x4) = e^(-H y - a4) (geometry.kappa), the good sector without extra-time momenta (sectorAndAnsatz.sector) and the status of the history a4 = A H x4 (a prescribed background, not solved for)
