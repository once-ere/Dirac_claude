## 1. What the notebook computes

It estimates how fast the three extra times would have to deflate before the instantaneous Kohn-Sham states of dirac16complex stop describing the gas: it reads the adiabaticity measure Q of the 75 recorded ground states and shows that Q grows exactly in proportion to the rate, reproduces Q of the free Fermi-shell sectors with the solver's shooting method, collapses every slice and shell onto one curve with the exact rescaling identity, measures the jumps into the negative-energy branch, and solves the exact time evolution of the free Fermi-shell sector of N = 688 in a basis of 20 instantaneous levels for rates from 0.25 to 251, compared with the first-order estimate and the sudden limit.
