The author asks for three analyses of the universe model UM-3: the mass
mechanism along the hidden coordinate \(x_0\) (the period \(\pi/(6H)\)
of the metric and the uncertainty relation), whether dirac16complex can
be the dark matter, and whether it can be the dark energy. He also asks
to be told what he may have left out. This document gives the four
answers. It is self-contained: every equation it uses is written in it
(with the tag it carries in every UM-3 document, FE-n for a field
equation, or A-n for an equation of this document), and every symbol of
every display equation is defined right after it. The sections are:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{The answers in brief.}
\item
  \textbf{The \(x_0\) mass mechanism}: the period of \(x_0\), the proper
  length of the hidden direction, the spectra that replace the tower of
  a compact dimension, and the uncertainty relation.
\item
  \textbf{dirac16complex as dark matter}: stability, gravitation on the
  fold, the equation of state of the Kohn-Sham states, the relic
  density, direct detection, and the window of parameters that survives.
\item
  \textbf{dirac16complex and the dark energy}: why the gas of bound
  states cannot be dark energy, what the dark energy of UM-3 is, and how
  it compares with DESI DR2.
\item
  \textbf{What the author may have left out}: the additions that UM-3
  needed, and the open problems.
\end{itemize}

mass by the uncertainty relation, because one period is an infinite
proper distance. The hidden direction produces masses in two other ways:
the warp gives every massless bulk field a gap \(3H\) above its zero

dirac16complex in eight states of mass

uncertainty relation makes lighter the narrower they are. Status:
DERIVED.
