\section{How to read this document}\label{how-to-read-this-document}}

This document derives and states the complete set of coupled field
equations of the universe model UM-2: the gravitational equations with
every source, the equations of every matter field with every
interaction, their boundary conditions, the exact background (order-0)
equations, the exact reductions to the hidden coordinate \(x_0\) that

equations that describe the CMB, the CNB and the BAO, and the equations
of the flavour evolution of the three neutrinos in vacuum, in matter, in
the early Universe and in dense neutrino gases. It is self-contained:
every equation it uses is written here. Labels: AUTHOR (the author's
notebooks), RECORD (the Revision record of this repository), STANDARD
(textbook), ASSUMED, INVENTED (new in UM-2), DERIVED (computed and
verified in UM-2; the verification is stated), ESTIMATE (an
order-of-magnitude evaluation), OPEN.

anomaly free generation by generation, with \(3\times3\) tip coupling
matrices, from which the mixing matrices follow as
\(U = \kappa_e^\dagger\kappa_\nu\) and \(V = \kappa_u^\dagger\kappa_d\)
(section 6.2); the flavour evolution of the neutrinos is section 9. The
chiral tip \(Z\) portal of version 1 is removed (section 2).

\hypertarget{vacuum-and-matter}{%

The flavour amplitudes \(\psi_\alpha\) of a neutrino of energy

\[ i\frac{d\psi}{dx_4} = \mathcal H\psi,\qquad \mathcal H = \frac{1}{2E}\,U\,\mathrm{diag}(m_1^2, m_2^2, m_3^2)\,U^\dagger + V , \]

\(P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta) = \lvert S_{\beta\alpha}\rvert^2\); for a

\[ P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta) = \Big\lvert\sum_iU_{\beta i}U^*_{\alpha i}\,e^{-im_i^2L/(2E)}\Big\rvert^2, \]

\(J = \mathrm{Im}(U_{e2}U_{\mu3}U^*_{e3}U^*_{\mu2}) = c_{12}s_{12}c_{23}s_{23}c_{13}^2s_{13}\sin\delta\)
and CPT,
\(P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta) = \bar P(\bar\nu_\beta\to\bar\nu_\alpha)\).

produced at a density where the effective 1-2 mixing angle is

averaged over the incoherent mass states, with
\[ P_{ee} = c_{13}^4\Big(\frac12 + \frac12\cos2\theta_{12}\cos2\theta^m_{12}\Big) + s_{13}^4,\qquad

\(n_e = \Delta m^2\cos2\theta/(2\sqrt2\,G_FE)\) is crossed adiabatically

\(H = \sqrt{8\pi\rho/3}/M_{Pl}\) of the total density (here \(H\) is not

GeV), the energy densities \(\rho_\gamma = \pi^2T_\gamma^4/15\) and
\(\rho_{\nu_\alpha} = 7\pi^2T_{\nu_\alpha}^4/120\) per flavour and the
\(e^\pm\) energy density \(\rho_e\) and pressure \(p_e\) (with the
electron mass, \(g = 4\), no chemical potential),
\[ \frac{dT_\gamma}{dx_4} = -\frac{4H\rho_\gamma + 3H(\rho_e + p_e) + 3HT_\gamma\frac{dP_{int}}{dT_\gamma} + \frac{\delta\rho_{\nu_e}}{\delta x_4} + 2\frac{\delta\rho_{\nu_\mu}}{\delta x_4}}
   {\frac{d\rho_\gamma}{dT_\gamma} + \frac{d\rho_e}{dT_\gamma} + T_\gamma\frac{d^2P_{int}}{dT_\gamma^2}},\qquad
   \frac{dT_{\nu_\alpha}}{dx_4} = -HT_{\nu_\alpha} + \frac{\delta\rho_{\nu_\alpha}/\delta x_4}{d\rho_{\nu_\alpha}/dT_{\nu_\alpha}}, \]
\[ \frac{\delta\rho_{\nu_e}}{\delta x_4} = \frac{G_F^2}{\pi^5}\Big[4(g_{eL}^2 + g_{eR}^2)F(T_\gamma, T_{\nu_e}) + 2F(T_{\nu_\mu}, T_{\nu_e})\Big],\qquad
   \frac{\delta\rho_{\nu_\mu}}{\delta x_4} = \frac{G_F^2}{\pi^5}\Big[4(g_{\mu L}^2 + g_{\mu R}^2)F(T_\gamma, T_{\nu_\mu}) - F(T_{\nu_\mu}, T_{\nu_e})\Big], \]
\[ F(T_1, T_2) = 32f_a(T_1^9 - T_2^9) + 56f_sT_1^4T_2^4(T_1 - T_2),\qquad f_a = 0.884,\quad f_s = 0.829 \]
(\(f_a\), \(f_s\) the Fermi-Dirac factors of the annihilation and
scattering rates; \(g_{eL} = \frac12 + s^2\), \(g_{eR} = s^2\) for
\(\nu_e\), which interacts with electrons through both currents, and
\(g_{\mu L} = -\frac12 + s^2\), \(g_{\mu R} = s^2\) for
\(\nu_{\mu,\tau}\), with \(s^2 = \sin^2\theta_W\), or the radiatively
corrected values \(0.727\), \(0.233\), \(-0.273\), \(0.233\)). When
oscillations equalise the flavours, one temperature \(T_\nu\) obeys
\(dT_\nu/dx_4 = -HT_\nu + (\delta\rho_{\nu_e}/\delta x_4 + 2\delta\rho_{\nu_\mu}/\delta x_4)/(3\,d\rho_\nu/dT_\nu)\).
The finite-temperature QED corrections enter through the interaction
pressure of the \(e^\pm\gamma\) plasma, \(P_{int} = P_2 + P_3\), and its
energy density \(\rho_{int} = -P_{int} + T\,dP_{int}/dT\) (STANDARD;
Bennett et al.~2020),
\[ P_2 = -\frac{e^2T^2}{12\pi^2}J_1 - \frac{e^2}{8\pi^4}J_1^2 + \frac{e^2m_e^2}{16\pi^4}J_2,\qquad P_3 = \frac{e^3T}{12\pi^4}I^{3/2}, \]
\[ J_1 = \int_0^\infty dp\,\frac{p^2}{E}n_D,\qquad J_2 = \int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty dp\,dq\,\frac{pq}{E_pE_q}\ln\Big\lvert\frac{p + q}{p - q}\Big\rvert n_D(p)n_D(q), \]
\[ I = \int_0^\infty dp\,\frac{p^2 + E^2}{E}n_D,\qquad n_D = \frac{2}{e^{E/T} + 1},\qquad E = \sqrt{p^2 + m_e^2} , \]
whose massless limits are \(P_2 = -5e^2T^4/288\) and
\(P_3 = e^3T^4/(36\sqrt3\,\pi)\). The effective number of neutrino
species is
\[ N_{eff} = \Big(\frac{11}{4}\Big)^{4/3}\Big[\Big(\frac{T_{\nu_e}}{T_\gamma}\Big)^4 + 2\Big(\frac{T_{\nu_\mu}}{T_\gamma}\Big)^4\Big] \]
after \(e^+e^-\) annihilation. For instantaneous decoupling at \(T_d\)
the plasma conserves its entropy \(a^3s\),
\(s = (\rho_\gamma + \frac13\rho_\gamma + \rho_e + p_e + \rho_{int} + P_{int})/T\),
while \(T_\nu \propto 1/a\).

\hypertarget{the-dense-neutrino-gas-the-fast-flavour-instability}{%
\subsection{The dense neutrino gas: the fast flavour
instability}\label{the-dense-neutrino-gas-the-fast-flavour-instability}}

Near a supernova core \(V_{\nu\nu}\) dominates \(\Omega_{\vec p}\). For
a homogeneous, axially symmetric gas of \(\nu_e\) and \(\bar\nu_e\) with
the angular distributions \(g_{\nu_e}(u)\), \(g_{\bar\nu_e}(u)\)
(\(u = \cos\vartheta\) along the symmetry axis,
\(\int_{-1}^{1}g\,du/2 = 1\)), the electron-lepton-number (ELN)
distribution
\[ G(u) = \sqrt2\,G_F\big[n_{\nu_e}g_{\nu_e}(u) - n_{\bar\nu_e}g_{\bar\nu_e}(u)\big] \]
governs the linearised evolution of the small flavour coherence \(S_u\)
(the \(e x\) element of \(\rho\), \(x\) a heavy-lepton flavour). With
\(v = (1, u)\), \(\Lambda^\mu = \int du/2\,G(u)\,v^\mu\) and modes
\(S_u \propto e^{-i(\Omega x_4 - Kx_3)}\) (\(x_3\) the symmetry axis) in
the shifted variables \(\omega = \Omega - \Lambda^0\),
\(k = K - \Lambda^3\), the linear equations have non-trivial solutions
if (STANDARD)
\[ \det\Pi(\omega, k) = 0,\qquad \Pi^{\mu\nu} = \eta^{\mu\nu} + \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{du}{2}\,G(u)\,\frac{v^\mu v^\nu}{\omega - ku}\qquad (\eta = \mathrm{diag}(1, -1)\ \text{on}\ (x_4, x_3)), \]
which splits into the axisymmetric branch and the azimuthal branch
\[ (1 + J_0)(-1 + J_2) - J_1^2 = 0,\quad J_n = \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{du}{2}\,\frac{G(u)\,u^n}{\omega - ku};\qquad -1 + \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{du}{2}\,\frac{G(u)\,(1 - u^2)}{2(\omega - ku)} = 0 . \]
A root with \(\mathrm{Im}\,\omega > 0\) for real \(k\) is an instability
with the growth rate \(\mathrm{Im}\,\omega\). The homogeneous mode
(\(K = 0\), i.e. \(k = -\Lambda^3\)) obeys
\[ i\frac{dS_u}{dx_4} = (\Lambda^0 - u\Lambda^3)\,S_u - \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{du'}{2}\,G(u')\,(1 - uu')\,S_{u'} , \]
and for the mode \(K = \Lambda^3\) (\(k = 0\)) the dispersion relation
is the quadratic
\(\omega^2 - (I_2 - I_0)\omega - (I_0I_2 - I_1^2) = 0\),
\(I_n = \int du/2\,G\,u^n\), unstable with the growth rate
\(\frac12\sqrt{4I_1^2 - (I_0 + I_2)^2}\) when this is real. An
instability exists only if \(G(u)\) changes sign (an ELN crossing;
Morinaga 2022).

\hypertarget{the-cosmic-neutrino-background}{%
\subsection{The cosmic neutrino
background}\label{the-cosmic-neutrino-background}}

After decoupling the neutrinos keep the Fermi-Dirac distribution
\(f(p) = 1/(e^{p/T_\nu} + 1)\) of their momentum, redshifted with
\(T_\nu \propto 1/a\), and lose their flavour coherence: the background
is an incoherent mixture of the mass states, each with the number
density and the energy density
\[ n_i = 2\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3}f(p) = \frac{3\zeta(3)}{2\pi^2}T_\nu^3,\qquad \rho_i = 2\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3}\sqrt{p^2 + m_i^2}\,f(p) \]
(the factor 2 counts \(\nu_i\) and \(\bar\nu_i\), one helicity each),
and the mean velocity
\(\langle v\rangle_i = \int p^2\,(p/\sqrt{p^2 + m_i^2})f\,dp/\int p^2f\,dp\).
The flavour content of the mass state \(\nu_i\) is
\(\lvert U_{\alpha i}\rvert^2\).
