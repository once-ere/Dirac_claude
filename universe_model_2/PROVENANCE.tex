\section{How to read this document}\label{how-to-read-this-document}}

This document records the provenance of UM-2, a model of the Universe
built on the author's 4+4-dimensional Einstein-Lovelock theory and his
dirac16complex field. For every ingredient it states where it comes
from, writes the ingredient out as an equation, names the computation
that produced or used it, and states how it was verified. The document
is self-contained: every equation it mentions is written here.

Labels used throughout:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{AUTHOR}: taken from the author's Mathematica notebooks or the
  author's written orders;
\item
  \textbf{RECORD}: taken from the Revision record of this repository
  (earlier work carried out under the author's orders, with its own
  verification);
\item
  \textbf{STANDARD}: textbook physics or a measured value; measured
  values are marked \emph{verified} (read from the source on 2026-10-17)
  or \emph{from memory} (quoted from memory of the standard tables, to
  be checked against the current editions);
\item
  \textbf{ASSUMED}: a modelling choice made for UM-2;
\item
  \textbf{INVENTED}: a new phenomenological interaction of UM-2;
\item
  \textbf{DERIVED}: computed and verified in UM-2;
\item
  \textbf{ESTIMATE}: an order-of-magnitude evaluation, not a precision
  computation;
\item
  \textbf{OPEN}: not resolved in this version.
\end{itemize}

Units: \(\hbar = c = 1\); \(H\) is the author's constant (an inverse
length); in the numerical work \(H = 1\) unless a physical unit is
stated.

Symbols with more than one use: \(k\) is the block variable \(1/f^2\),
the Lovelock order as the index of \(\alpha_k\) and \(E_{(k)}\), and the
momentum of a mode along \(x_1, x_2, x_3\) in \(e^{ikx_1}\) and \(h_j\);
\(\Lambda = -10H^2\) is the eight-dimensional cosmological constant,
\(\Lambda = (m\,fL)^2\) in the \(x_0\) eigenvalue problems a

effective interactions; \(\xi = y/L\) is the scaled hidden coordinate
(primes denote \(d/d\xi\) in the methods), while the subscript \(t\)
(\(p_t\), \(v_t\), \(x_t\)) refers to the extra times and \(s_h\),
\(t_h\) are the \(4\times4\) blocks of the gamma matrices; \(\rho\) is
an energy density, except for the electroweak parameter
\(\rho = \Lambda_W/(\Lambda_Z\cos^2\theta_W)\) (measured value
\(\rho_0\)), and \(\varrho\) a probability density in \(\xi\);
\(\theta_W\) is the angle of the gauge couplings; \(n\) is a number

is the radion in the metric expansion and, with indices, an uncertainty

\subsection{Methods, with the equations they
use}\label{methods-with-the-equations-they-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Exact checks.} Symbolic identities are verified with sympy by
  simplifying the difference to zero; matrix identities entry by entry.
\item
  \textbf{Boson eigenvalues.} With \(\xi = y/L\),
  \(u = e^{-3\eta\xi/2}\tilde u\), \(q^2 = \Lambda - 9\eta^2/4\), the
  operator \(-e^{-3\eta\xi}(e^{3\eta\xi}u')' = \Lambda u\) with
  \(u'(0) = 0\) (even) or \(u(0) = 0\) (odd) and
  \(u'(-1) = \beta u(-1)\) has the eigenvalue conditions
  \(\big[q^2 + \tfrac{3\eta}{2}(\beta + \tfrac{3\eta}{2})\big]\sin q - \beta q\cos q = 0\)
  (even) and \(q\cos q + (\beta + \tfrac{3\eta}{2})\sin q = 0\) (odd),
  solved with mpmath at 30 digits. Cross-check: quadratic finite
  elements for
  \(\int e^{3\eta\xi}u'^2d\xi + \beta e^{-3\eta}u(-1)^2 = \Lambda\int e^{3\eta\xi}u^2d\xi\)
  on 100, 200, 400 elements, the eigenvalue taken from the Rayleigh
  quotient of the gradient form and extrapolated by Richardson.
\item
  \textbf{Richardson extrapolation.} For a quantity with error
  \(C\,n^{-p}\): \(x \approx x_{2n} + (x_{2n} - x_n)/(2^p - 1)\); the
  observed order is \(\log_2[(x_n - x_{2n})/(x_{2n} - x_{4n})]\).
\item
  \textbf{Fermion eigenvalues.} The exact condition
  \(D(e) = e^2s_Ls_R - \kappa_f^2(C_L - c_Ls_L)(C_R + c_Rs_R)\),
  \(s = \sinh w/w\), \(C = \cosh w\), \(w = \sqrt{c^2 - e^2}\), solved
  with mpmath; the first-order solution
  \(e = \kappa_f\zeta(c_L)\zeta(-c_R)\),
  \(\zeta(c)^2 = c\,e^{-c}/\sinh c\) (\(c_L > 0\), \(c_R < 0\));
  cross-check by direct integration of the coupled \((L, R)\) Dirac
  system (DOP853, relative tolerance \(10^{-13}\)).
\item
  \textbf{Kohn-Sham.} Orbitals of \(h_j\) are expanded in the exact
  eigenfunctions of the free problem of each sector (60, 120 or 240
  functions, quadrature with ten points per function), the mean fields
  \(M_{eff} = m + \tfrac{15}{16}\lambda S\),
  \(v = -\tfrac{1}{16}\lambda n\) are iterated to self-consistency

  for every portal mean field, and the reproduction of the record's two
  reference states to \(10^{-13}\) (energies, HOMO, LUMO) and
  \(10^{-9}\) (integrated hidden pressure).
\item

  \(\dot k = -2kh\) and the stabiliser \(\kappa\rho_b \propto k^4\); the
