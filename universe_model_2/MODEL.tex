\hypertarget{the-flavour-evolution}{%
\subsection{The flavour evolution}\label{the-flavour-evolution}}

At energies far below \(m_W\) the charged and neutral currents
(catalogue rows 7 and 8) act as the four-fermion interaction with
\(G_F/\sqrt2 = g^2/(8m_W^2)\) (STANDARD; UM-2 uses the measured
\(G_F = 1.1663788\times10^{-5}\) GeV\(^{-2}\)). Coherent forward
scattering in matter then adds potentials to the free propagation, and
the flavour amplitudes \(\psi_\alpha\) of a neutrino of energy \(E\)
evolve as (STANDARD)
\[ i\frac{d\psi}{dt} = \mathcal H\psi,\qquad \mathcal H = \frac{1}{2E}\,U\,\mathrm{diag}(m_1^2, m_2^2, m_3^2)\,U^\dagger + \mathrm{diag}(V_{CC}, 0, 0) + V_{NC}\,1, \]
\[ V_{CC} = \sqrt2\,G_Fn_e,\qquad V_{NC} = -\frac{G_Fn_n}{\sqrt2} \]
(\(n_e\), \(n_n\) the electron and neutron densities; \(V_{NC}\) is
flavour universal and drops out of the probabilities); for antineutrinos
\(U \to U^*\) and \(V_{CC}, V_{NC} \to -V_{CC}, -V_{NC}\). In vacuum
\[ P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta) = \Big\lvert\sum_iU_{\beta i}U^*_{\alpha i}\,e^{-im_i^2L/(2E)}\Big\rvert^2,\qquad P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta) = \bar P(\nu_\beta\to\nu_\alpha)\ \text{(CPT)},\]
\[ P(\nu_\mu\to\nu_e) - P(\nu_e\to\nu_\mu) = -16J\sin\Delta_{21}\sin\Delta_{31}\sin\Delta_{32},\qquad \Delta_{ij} = \frac{\Delta m^2_{ij}L}{4E}. \]
In a dense neutrino gas (the early Universe, a supernova core) the state
of the neutrinos of momentum \(\vec p\) is a \(3\times3\) density matrix
\(\rho_{\vec p}\) (and \(\bar\rho_{\vec p}\) for antineutrinos), which
obeys the quantum kinetic equation (STANDARD)
\[ i(\partial_t + \vec v_p\cdot\nabla)\rho_{\vec p} = \big[\Omega_{\vec p} + \mathrm{diag}(V_{CC}, 0, 0) + V_{\nu\nu}, \rho_{\vec p}\big] + i\,\mathcal C[\rho],\qquad
   \Omega_{\vec p} = \frac{U\,\mathrm{diag}(m_i^2)\,U^\dagger}{2p}, \]
\[ V_{\nu\nu} = \sqrt2\,G_F\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}\,(1 - \vec v_p\cdot\vec v_q)\big(\rho_{\vec q} - \bar\rho_{\vec q}\big), \]
with \(\vec v_p = \vec p/p\) and \(\mathcal C\) the collision term.

\textbf{The fast flavour instability.} Near the neutrinosphere the
neutrino gas is dense enough for \(V_{\nu\nu}\) to dominate. For an
axially symmetric gas with the electron-lepton-number (ELN) angular
distribution
\(G(u) = \sqrt2\,G_F[n_{\nu_e}g_{\nu_e}(u) - n_{\bar\nu_e}g_{\bar\nu_e}(u)]\)
(\(u = \cos\vartheta\), \(\int_{-1}^{1}g\,du/2 = 1\)), the linearised
equations for the flavour coherence \(S_u\) give plane-wave modes
\(e^{-i(\Omega t - Kz)}\) with \(\omega = \Omega - \Lambda_0\),
\(k = K - \Lambda_3\), \(\Lambda^\mu = \int du/2\,G\,v^\mu\),
\(v = (1, u)\), and the dispersion relation (STANDARD)
\[ \det\Pi = 0,\qquad \Pi^{\mu\nu} = \eta^{\mu\nu} + \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{du}{2}\,G(u)\,\frac{v^\mu v^\nu}{\omega - ku}, \]
whose axisymmetric branch is \((1 + J_0)(-1 + J_2) - J_1^2 = 0\),
\(J_n = \int du/2\,G\,u^n/(\omega - ku)\), and whose azimuthal branch is
\(-1 + \int du/2\,G(1 - u^2)/(2(\omega - ku)) = 0\);
\(\mathrm{Im}\,\omega > 0\) is an instability. In units of
\(\mu = \sqrt2G_Fn_{\nu_e}\) (DERIVED): with an ELN crossing
(forward-peaked antineutrinos,
\(\alpha = n_{\bar\nu_e}/n_{\nu_e} = 0.8\)) the largest growth rate is

distributions (\(g_{\nu_e} = 1 + 0.2u\), \(g_{\bar\nu_e} = 1 + 0.6u\),
\(\alpha = 0.9\)) it is \(0.098\,\mu\) although the homogeneous mode is
stable; without a crossing no mode grows, as the general theorem
(Morinaga 2022) states. At \(n_{\nu_e} = 10^{32}\) to \(10^{33}\)
cm\(^{-3}\) the instability grows by \(e\) in \(6\times10^{-10}\) to
\(6\times10^{-11}\) s over \(18\) to \(1.8\) cm, a million times faster
than the hydrodynamics.

\textbf{Flavour conversion and the explosions.} Conversion moves energy
