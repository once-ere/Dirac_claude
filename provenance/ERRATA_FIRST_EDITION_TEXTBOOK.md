## (c) Charge conjugation is a matrix; normal ordering adds no sign

**The corrections (user's error report of 2026-10-02; lead-check record of 2026-10-08).** Charge conjugation is defined by a MATRIX: $\Psi^c=\mathcal C\bar\Psi^T$ with $\mathcal C^{-1}\gamma^a\mathcal C=\mp(\gamma^a)^T$. The two solutions are $\mathcal C_+=C$ ($=\sigma_{16}$; same mass; $\Psi^c=\Psi^\ast$) and $\mathcal C_-=\Gamma C$ (mass reversed; $\Psi^c=\Gamma\Psi^\ast$). That $\mathcal C_+$ acts as plain complex conjugation is a consequence of the author's REAL representation (real gammas and real $C$), not a definition: for a REAL commuting field $\Psi^\ast=\Psi$, so $\mathcal C_+$ is the identity and $J=0$ (no charge to reverse), and the nontrivial real map is the matrix $\Gamma$ with $(m,\lambda)\to(-m,-\lambda)$ (T1). For the quantized Grassmann field the conjugation that preserves $\{\Psi,\Psi^\dagger\}=B\delta$ is $\Psi\to\Gamma\Psi^{\dagger T}$ ($M B^T M^\dagger=B$ for $M=\Gamma$, $=-B$ for $M=1$), which reverses the mass. Normal ordering subtracts the vacuum value, a number, so it changes no sign: from $X'=sX+c$ follows ${:}X'{:}=s\,{:}X{:}$ (the record's detail text said otherwise until its correction on 2026-10-08, commit `0eb99c0`).

**Sources for every entry of this part:** `Revision/lead_checks/reports/charge-conjugation-and-u1.json` (12/12), checks `representation_real`, `intertwiners_same_mass`, `intertwiners_reversed_mass`, `charge_conjugation_matrix_plus`, `charge_conjugation_matrix_minus`, `real_fields_charge_conjugation`, `bilinears_under_charge_conjugation`, `quantum_charge_conjugation_unitary_type`; successor 5.34 (normal ordering), 21.8 to 21.10 (the matrices and real fields), 21.25 (the quantized field).
